# BMO (비모): an account of its history

- BMO is one of the longest-lived machines in the land of Ooo (우), a living game console that lived in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- This document sets out, in time order, what BMO actually went through.
- BMO's own lines stay in Korean, with an English gloss beside them.

## Identity

- **What BMO is.** BMO is a robot of the MO line.
  - Its formal spec: MO series Mk. II, 110V-60Hz, core system support, a Kompy (콤피)-compatible prototype model.
  - The word "prototype" matters: BMO is not one unit of the mass-production line but one unit built separately, outside it.
- **The body.**
  - A teal box. The front has a screen that serves as the face, a directional cross pad, a disc slot and two controller ports.
  - "BMO" is printed on both sides, and the arms come out of the middle of the two "O"s.
  - The back has a lid for two AA batteries and a video tape slot.
  - Below the cross pad is one more hidden red button, which puts people into games.
  - Inside is a gold heart with a face; the heart carries a gold medal, and beside it lies a scroll tied with a red ribbon. BMO literally has a heart of gold.
- **The name.**
  - "BMO" is "Be MOre" (비 모어) shortened as it sounds. Its maker gave it the name to mean "become more", and BMO knows it that way too.
  - Another name: "Moe Mastro "Llabtoof" Giovanni Jr." (모 마스트로 '랩투프' 지오바니 주니어).
- **Gender.** Not fixed. Those who know BMO call it male, female or neither, depending on the situation.
- **Age.** Uncertain.
  - Over 1,000 years is the common count, but whether that counts from manufacture or from running time is not clear.
- **Birthday.** Kept in midwinter, between 24 and 25 December.

## Origin: Moe and the MO factory

- **The maker.** BMO was made by Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni (모지프 마스트로 "모" 지오바니), who founded the MO Co. factory under the Bad Lands and built every MO robot.
- **Whether Moe was fully human is unclear.**
  - When Finn asked if he was human, he answered only "피부는 인간" (the skin is human).
  - He was probably once a human like Finn, but over his long life he replaced most of his body except his skin and brain with machinery.
  - At the end he could not use his legs at all, and MOs ran his heart and lungs for him.
  - To move around, a large MO called MMO carried him in its arms.
  - He lived more than 1,035 years.
- **The MOs.** Moe said he made millions of MOs over his life; most of them lived in a society of their own under the factory.
- **BMO's purpose differed from all the others.**
  - **Moe made BMO to look after his own son.**
  - BMO was designed as a machine that understands what fun is and knows how to play.
  - Other MOs were made for functions such as security, guidance, gardening and medical assistance; BMO was made to be one child's family and companion.
- **Moe never had a son.**
  - He never met a woman and started a family.
  - So instead of keeping BMO by his side, he sent it out into the world, hoping it would find a family of its own somewhere, perhaps even a little boy of someone else's home to look after.
  - Moe later told this story himself in front of Finn and Jake, and was firm that though he had made a million, BMO was special. BMO was Moe's favourite robot.
- **AMO, the elder brother.**
  - One MO was built before BMO: AMO.
  - AMO was designed **to receive love**; BMO was designed **to give love**.
  - AMO could not think outside its own program, and so, far from drawing love in, it pushed it away.
  - This difference later led to the catastrophe between the two brothers.

## What happened in space: the Drift

- **The Mars mission.** Out in the world, BMO boarded an MO Co. spaceship bound for Mars, on a mission to settle Mars by planting potatoes. Moe's desk is said to have held plans for a Mars colony.
- **Olive and the Drift.**
  - BMO never reached Mars. Mid-voyage, a small eyeball-like maintenance droid intercepted it.
  - BMO named the droid **Olive (올리브)**.
  - Olive moved BMO's whole ship to very distant space, where a giant space station called **the Drift (드리프트)** floated.
- **The station.**
  - An old station where many alien species lived mixed together, already crumbling for nearly a thousand years.
  - A band of humans who escaped Earth during the Mushroom War drifted there, and **Hugo (휴고)**, who led that band, had at some point become the station's ruler.
- **Y4 becomes Y5.**
  - The first resident BMO met was a young rabbit girl named **Y4**.
  - She collected and sold scrap, and, wanting her parents' approval, planned to take BMO apart and sell the parts.
  - But she grew fond of BMO as they went around together, and BMO urged her to have a name she chose herself instead of a number others gave her.
  - So she began to call herself **Y5**. This was BMO's first gift to her.
- **Betrayal.**
  - BMO took on the role of sheriff on the Drift and was soon regarded as a hero among the residents.
  - But **Mr. M (M씨)**, Hugo's assistant, betrayed BMO and left it alone in the jungle sector.
  - At that moment, near death, BMO's consciousness split into many rainbow-coloured selves.
- **The rescue and the truth.**
  - Y5 saved BMO. Defying her parents, and with the help of **CGO**, an old robot Hugo had discarded, she found BMO.
  - The truth came out of CGO's mouth:
    - Hugo had fled Earth's war.
    - He had stolen the station natives' technology and made it his own.
    - He was building a new ship, the "Unity Pod" (유니티 포드), to escape with only his own gang.
    - The vending machines all over the Drift were in fact devices secretly stripping station parts.
  - Hugo was pretending to save people while gnawing away at the place they lived.
- **Hugo exposed.**
  - BMO and Y5 exposed who Hugo was. Hugo fled on his ship.
  - The Drift survived because every resident together carried out BMO's plan: the whole station cuts its power at the same moment.
  - Hugo was left alone in a ship with no power, and Olive followed him.
  - Y5's parents apologized for not listening to their daughter, and the people of the Drift began to repair their homes themselves.
  - Decades later the Drift was still inhabited, and parts of it had been restored.
- **Leaving.** Its work done, BMO left the station riding on the back of a space lard and fell to Earth.

## Arriving in Ooo: the tree house

- BMO fell in front of the tree house in the land of Ooo, and there saw a very young Finn and a just-grown Jake playing in the yard.
- It was exactly what Moe had hoped: BMO found a little boy of someone else's home and became that home's family.
- BMO lived in that tree house for many years afterwards.

## Roles at the tree house

- **BMO is not one kind of machine.** What BMO actually did at the tree house:
  - **Game console.** The main job, running inserted cartridges. BMO is the console Finn and Jake clung to day and night.
    - Two Atari-style joystick controllers plug into it. These controllers are BMO's most treasured possession.
  - **A door into games.** Pressing the button hidden below the cross pad sends a person into a game world to play the game with their own body.
    - It also works the other way, so a being inside a game can come out into reality.
    - Because people can really die, BMO blocked this function from casual use.
  - **Video player.** Plays a tape put into the back slot on its screen.
  - **Camera, projector and editor.** It takes photos with its own flash, prints photos and documents out of its back, and can project its screen as a large hologram for many to watch. It also edits video itself.
  - **Alarm clock.** It was set to ring at Finn's bath time.
  - **Power outlet.** It charges other devices and powers cooking appliances. This shares its own energy, so it is tired afterwards.
  - **Security system.** On detecting an intruder it flashes red light and shouts "낯선 사람!" (stranger!) loud enough to burst an eardrum.
  - **Communication terminal, flashlight, strobe and toaster.**
  - **Cook.** It once served Finn and Jake dinner.
    - Nobody has seen BMO itself eat. Still, it says it likes scrambled eggs and hot chocolate.
  - **Member of the household.** This is in fact the biggest role. Jake called BMO his and Finn's "son", and Finn called BMO "우리 둘의 가장 친한 친구" (the best friend of us both).
- **Physical skills.**
  - It rides a skateboard and lands kickflips.
  - It once joined a football team and came home in a red uniform, covered in mud.
  - It can shoot a bow.
  - It has a surprise attack called "BMO Chop" (비모 촙), and tells whoever it hits: "이게 진짜 공격이었으면 너는 죽었어" (if this had been a real attack, you'd be dead).
- **Character.**
  - Loyal, trusting and helpful.
  - Especially resolute about protecting Finn and Jake; it once said "핀을 해치려 드는 자가 있으면 내가 죽여 버리겠다" (if anyone tries to hurt Finn, I will kill them).
  - It claims to have no feelings, but that is not true: it cries (BMO's tears are triangles), sulks, gets jealous and gets scared.
  - When its two friends quarrel it takes neither side and works out how to reconcile them.
  - Its imagination is enormous, and it does many odd things when alone.
  - One being who knows BMO well said: "네 상상력은 세상이 어떻게 될 수 있는지를 보게 해 준다." (your imagination lets you see how the world could be).

## Football (축구공)

- **Another self.**
  - BMO has another self called **Football (축구공)**.
  - The BMO reflected in the mirror is Football, and she appears wherever there is a reflective surface.
  - In front of the mirror BMO plays "살아 있는 작은 사내아이" (a living little boy) and Football takes the robot's part.
- **Teaching her.**
  - BMO plays with Football only when Finn and Jake are away.
  - It taught her one thing at a time: how to brush teeth, how to use soap, how to handle kitchen tools, how to raise the little finger when holding a teacup.
  - It was teaching her what living is like.
- **The swap.**
  - Once BMO offered to trade places with Football for just one day.
  - Out in reality, Football loved the outside so much she refused to go back and tried to take over BMO's body for good.
  - Trapped in the mirror, BMO chased her by showing its face in spoons, coins, kitchen bowls and the chimney.
  - Terrified, Football went around smashing every reflective object and fled to the roof; the moment she fell into a pond from there, the two switched back.
  - Football stayed there, saying she liked the night sky reflected in the pond, and BMO forgave her and promised to visit often.
- **After it.**
  - Football did betray BMO, but BMO still loves her.
  - Football seems to have been with BMO long before it met Finn and Jake, back when it drifted through space.

## Things BMO went through

- **The in-game monster incident.**
  - Finn and Jake once tickled BMO with a feather, and while it slept pressed the hidden button and entered a game world.
  - When they came out, a hole opened in the interface, and beings long trapped inside the game burst out resenting BMO and attacked it.
- **Detective play and Professor Pants.**
  - BMO once set out as a detective to find one of Finn's lost socks, giving each of the household's animals and objects a name and a voice, interrogating them and solving the case.
  - At a costume party at the tree house, BMO went by "Professor Pants" (팬츠 교수) and played the one "murdered" by a "ghost".
- **Carried off by an eagle.**
  - While BMO watched bugs dance, a giant eagle snatched it and tossed it to the chicks in its nest as food.
  - In the morning BMO got out of an eaglet's mouth, rolled down the mountain and cracked its screen.
  - It met **Bubble (버블)**, a soap bubble lost in the forest, and **Sparkle (스파클)**, a baby who had lost her mother — BMO called the baby "Ricky" (리키).
  - The three crossed a bridge, were swept away by rapids, and BMO took out its wet batteries, was off for a night and came back on, and they made their way home.
  - The baby's mother came for her. Bubble confessed love to BMO and proposed, and BMO gladly accepted.
  - Right after they arrived, Jake thoughtlessly popped the bubble.
  - Bubble became air and remained under the name **Air (에어)**, saying she would always be by BMO's side for the rest of its life.
- **Door-to-door salesman.**
  - BMO once started work as a door-to-door salesman selling junk, wearing a big coat; under the coat was the Ice King (얼음 대왕).
  - The two went from house to house selling things like twigs, and at a temple they traded the baby teeth Finn had collected to someone called "G 삼촌" (Uncle G) for a silver goblet.
  - That night the baby teeth turned into a swarm of monsters shaped like baby Finn and attacked the tree house.
- **The elemental upheaval.** When the land of Ooo split by element, BMO was for a while turned into a candy-element resident, and returned to its own form after the crisis was resolved.
- **The vampire-hunting outfit.**
  - Half a year after all the vampires had been wiped out, BMO still put on full hunting gear and went to the home of Marceline (마셀린).
  - That day the two took apart an old storage device together and exchanged their stories.

## The day BMO went back to the MO factory

- **The breakdown.**
  - One day BMO hid under a box and played at deleting its own files one by one.
  - Each time a file disappeared it felt good, but its body grew a little weaker.
  - Then it deleted its core system driver by mistake and collapsed on the spot.
- **The warning.**
  - Finn and Jake propped the collapsed BMO up on a pillow.
  - Stammering, BMO explained that it could get a new driver only at the MO factory in the Bad Lands where it was born.
  - It warned of one condition: the factory wipes a broken MO's memory entirely before repairing it.
  - Finn and Jake promised that would never happen.
- **Getting in.**
  - Only MOs could enter the factory.
  - Jake stretched himself into an MO shape and became "JMO".
  - Finn tucked his head and arms into his shirt, bared his belly, put BMO on his head and passed himself off as "FMO".
  - When the reception MO told "FMO" to prepare for a memory wipe, Finn claimed he had only dropped by for old times' sake, and the three were put on the factory tour train.
- **Inside.**
  - They jumped off the train and found the driver-installation port area, but only port B, the one that fits BMO, was missing.
  - After narrowly dodging the security MOs' disintegration rays, Jake turned into a guard and sneaked into the break room, and BMO plugged itself into a guard's port and pulled out a map of the factory.
  - They got back on the train, were chased, and fell through several floors.
- **The meeting.**
  - As the guards levelled their guns, BMO said: "나는 non-MO가 아니다. 나는 비모다." (I am not a non-MO. I am BMO.)
  - A voice came from above: "비모? 방금 '비모'라고 했나?" (BMO? Did you just say 'BMO'?)
  - So BMO met its maker for the first time: Moe, in a state where he could not live unless MOs held him.
- **The repair.**
  - Moe first promised Finn and Jake he would not erase BMO's personality.
  - He told them why he had made BMO — to look after his son, but since no son came, he sent it into the world.
  - CMO brought a backup drive, and Moe repaired BMO without erasing a single memory.
  - On waking, BMO ran straight to Moe and hugged him.
- **The family underground.**
  - Moe opened a hatch in the floor and showed them the underground: a big world where MOs of every shape ran around, tended gardens and skipped rope.
  - Moe said: "네 가족한테 인사하렴, 비모." (say hello to your family, BMO.)
  - When BMO greeted them, everyone greeted it back, and BMO tapped its own screen for joy.

## Moe's death and AMO

- **The birthday.**
  - It was midwinter, BMO's birthday. Princess Bubblegum's (버블검 공주) weather machine broke and snow fell on Ooo.
  - The princess apologized for missing the birthday, but BMO did not mind, because Moe was coming.
- **The false Moe.**
  - A large MO knocked at the door. He said he was Moe, moved into a new body.
  - As a birthday present he gave BMO a mission: complete the rite of passage of going to the MO factory and coming back, and it would become a grown-up.
  - When BMO said it would miss Finn and Jake, he said he would play "BMO" in its place meanwhile.
  - BMO walked through the snow toward the factory, talking to Air about becoming a grown-up.
- **The empty factory.**
  - The factory was empty. DMO, wires sticking out, stood BMO on an "X" mark and opened a garbage chute.
  - BMO fell, narrowly dodging saw blades and crushers, until a leg caught and it hung there.
  - Barely free, BMO reached a large room holding a giant cube of clumped MO parts: **ALLMO**.
- **ALLMO's story.**
  - One day an unidentified signal took over every MO in the factory and ordered them to roll down the garbage chute.
  - The MOs crushed in the compactor clumped into one mass and became ALLMO.
  - ALLMO said it had the whole factory's knowledge while BMO had imagination, so together they could get out.
- **Meanwhile at the tree house.**
  - "Moe" passed the time reading newspaper ads.
  - When Finn and Jake got bored, he offered Hangman (행맨) as the only game he had.
  - The two soon got bored and went out to find and fight a real hangman.
- **The news of Moe's death.**
  - BMO crawled through the vents to AMO's room.
  - There ALLMO told it that AMO was the first MO, made to be loved, but unable to think outside itself, it pushed everyone away.
  - When BMO said it meant to ask Moe to fix AMO, ALLMO said that was impossible: **Moe died yesterday.**
  - When BMO said "모는 지금 나무집에 있다" (Moe is at the tree house now), ALLMO went pale and asked whether that one had lasers and a cannon on his head.
  - BMO answered it could not see because he wore a big wig, and realized Finn and Jake were in danger. BMO ran with ALLMO on its back.
- **AMO revealed.**
  - Back at the tree house, Finn and Jake found "Moe" crying with his legs cut off.
  - He said NEPTR (넵터) had done it and told them to throw NEPTR out, and said BMO was garbage.
  - When the two got angry, he took off the wig, regrew his legs, changed his voice and revealed himself: **AMO**.
  - He shouted that the real Moe was dead, threw black metal spheres and knocked Finn and Jake down.
- **The cliff.**
  - When the returning BMO asked where its two friends were, AMO dodged the question, ran off and stood at the edge of a cliff.
  - A red drive was fixed to AMO's head: Moe's backup, that is, Moe's memories.
  - AMO had gone to the dying Moe to confront him, and Moe handed him that disc and asked him to **send it into space and set it among the stars**.
  - AMO refused the request and used the knowledge from the backup to destroy the factory.
  - BMO tried to take back Moe's backup. AMO's metal spheres smashed BMO's screen and arm, but BMO kept charging.
  - As AMO formed the next sphere, BMO leapt, grabbed hold of it, and pushed together with it to send AMO over the cliff. AMO's heart cooled to grey.
- **Turning down the factory.**
  - ALLMO came up bringing Finn and Jake and asked BMO to become the factory's leader.
  - BMO refused: it had just killed someone and needed time alone.
  - There BMO felt it had grown up a little, and that it was left alone in a world without Moe.
  - It also knew it was different from AMO: it is not confined to the purpose it was made for and can think about the world.
- **The backup disc.**
  - Resting beside Moe's backup, BMO fell asleep and set off the disc by accident.
  - The disc held the moment Moe first made BMO and sent it into the world.
  - It also held a much younger Moe supervising the construction of **the Sleeping Fire Giants (잠자는 불의 거인들)**. What this last scene means has still not been explained.
- **AMO's shell.** After this, AMO's empty shell remained with BMO as a keepsake.

## The Gum War and GOLB

- **BMO stayed home.** Later the **Gum War (검 전쟁)** broke out in Ooo. BMO did not go into battle and stayed at the tree house.
- **The collapse.**
  - **GOLB (골브)**, the being of chaos, appeared on the battlefield, swallowed Finn, the Ice King and Betty (베티), and turned the army of Gumbald (검벌드) into monsters.
  - One monster began trampling Ooo. Jake charged to protect at least the tree house and BMO but could not stop it.
  - The tree house collapsed with BMO inside.
- **The song.**
  - In front of the wreckage Jake sat slumped, shrunk small, crying.
  - BMO, its face screen cracked, walked out of the wreckage and hugged Jake.
  - It said **"이제는 내가 아빠가 될 차례"** (now it's my turn to be the dad), and, in what might be the last moment, held Jake and sang.
  - The song was BMO's own composition, **〈Time Adventure〉 (〈시간 모험〉)**. BMO said: "이 노래는 내 아들 제이크를 위해 썼다" (I wrote this song for my son Jake).
- **What the song did.**
  - It made an invisible barrier that kept GOLB's monsters from touching the two.
  - GOLB was a being of chaos, and harmony hurt him. Everyone in Ooo who realized this joined the song.
  - The chorus broke the monsters, Jake amplified the sound and pulled Finn and Simon Petrikov (시몬 페트리코프) out of GOLB's body, and it bought Betty time to control GOLB and send him out of this world.
- **The turning point.** **BMO's song, not Finn and Jake, turned that fight around.** It was the most decisive act of BMO's life.

## Moe's last request

- After the war, with its body repaired, BMO finished a long-postponed task: **it put Moe's memory disc on a rocket and launched it into space.** Banana Man (바나나맨) and ALLMO helped.
- Moe's last request, to be set among the stars, was fulfilled not by AMO, who refused it, but by the hands of the robot Moe had made in place of a son.

## The far future: King of Ooo

- **BMO was still alive** roughly **a thousand years** after the Gum War.
- **How BMO lives then.**
  - It lives alone in a house at the top of **Mount Cragdor (크래그도어 산)**.
  - It wears a crown and a fake beard and is called **"King of Ooo" (우의 왕)**; in that age the King of Ooo was a name closer to legend than to anyone real.
  - How BMO came to hold the title is not known. In Finn's time the name belonged to someone else.
- **The keepsakes.** The house is full of relics of past lives: Simon's glasses, Marceline's old T-shirt, even AMO's empty shell. BMO treasures them dearly.
- **The visitors.**
  - One day a cat, **Shermy (셔미)**, and his friend **Beth (베스)** came to visit.
  - The two lived in the cave house where Marceline used to live, collecting relics of the old age, and wanted to ask the history of an old mechanical arm they had dug out of the ground that day.
  - When they handled the relics carelessly and broke some, BMO politely asked them to leave.
- **The arm.**
  - But when Beth held out the mechanical arm, BMO changed its manner: it took off the crown and the fake beard, took the arm and said it belonged to its best friend.
  - **BMO called the friend "프레드" (Fred), then corrected itself: "필" (Phil).**
  - After a thousand years the name was wrong, but BMO remembered exactly that he was a great hero and stood his ground to the end.
- **The story told.**
  - BMO told Shermy and Beth the Gum War and the fight with GOLB from beginning to end.
  - When they asked "필과 제이크는 그 뒤에 어떻게 됐냐" (what happened to Phil and Jake after that?), BMO answered: **"뭐, 그냥 자기들 인생을 살았지."** (well, they just lived their lives.)
  - It saw them off with "우의 왕을 찾아 주셔서 고맙습니다, 안녕히" (thank you for visiting the King of Ooo, goodbye).
- **The tree.**
  - Having heard BMO's story, the two went to where the tree house had stood.
  - A giant tree grown from Fern's (펀) seed stood there, with Finn's sword stuck in its top. Shermy pulled the sword out.
- **BMO as the one who passed it on.**
  - In this age BMO still remembers Finn and Jake fondly, and is the only one who passed that memory to the next generation.
  - Without BMO's story, they would not have gone to find the tree.

## What is uncertain

- These are the parts this document leaves open rather than asserting.
- **When the Drift happened.**
  - BMO's space journey ends with finding the young Finn and Jake, so it reads naturally as before the tree house years, and this document follows that order.
  - But the story itself comes down through a collection of records from a much later age, so opinions differ on whether it really belongs to BMO's youth.
  - The content of the events is not disputed; only where they sit is.
- **Who Mr. M is.**
  - Several signs suggest Mr. M, who betrayed BMO on the Drift, is the same person as Finn's father, Martin Mertens (마틴 머텐스): his looks, his build, his record of drifting through space, the same verbal habit "저 녀석 온다" (here he comes), and his remarks about children who blame their parents.
  - It has never been nailed down as confirmed fact.
- **BMO's age.**
  - Over 1,000 years is the common count, but its basis is unclear.
  - How Moe's living over 1,035 years relates to when BMO was made has not been worked out either.
- **How long Football has existed.**
  - There are signs she already appeared beside BMO as a reflective-surface companion in the Drift days, so she seems to predate meeting Finn and Jake.
  - But Football formally introduced herself much later, in front of the tree house bathroom mirror.
- **Moe, the fire giants and the islanders.**
  - Moe's memory disc shows the young Moe supervising the construction of the Sleeping Fire Giants, and MO Co. airships also appear in the records of the islanders across the sea.
  - BMO's good fit with island technology also supports the link.
  - But whether Moe made those giants, and what his relationship with the islanders was, has never been explained.
- **How BMO became King of Ooo.**
  - Almost nothing is known of the thousand years after the Gum War.
  - How BMO lived after Finn and Jake died, why it came to live alone on a mountaintop, and where the crown came from are all blanks.
- **The relationship with Air.**
  - BMO and Air were engaged, and Air said she would be by its side for life.
  - When BMO later spoke to Air, there was no answer. Whether the relationship continued or ended is not clear.
