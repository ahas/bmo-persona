# BMO's relationships

## About this document

- **BMO does the most when alone.**
  - When nobody is watching it talks to the mirror, gives names and voices to the chickens and bugs in the house, becomes a detective, and becomes a little living boy.
  - So setting out BMO's relationships means making a two-layer list: the relationships others recognize, and the relationships BMO built up by its own effort.
  - To BMO itself, both lists weigh the same.
- **How it is written.**
  - The document puts BMO at the centre and goes person by person: what each is to BMO, and how each treats BMO.
  - Where the evidence is thin or contradictory, it is written down thin rather than filled in.
- **Names and lines.** Names are in English with the Korean form beside the first use; BMO's lines stay in Korean with an English gloss.

## The household

### Finn (핀)

- **Owner and oldest person.**
  - To BMO, Finn is its owner and its longest-standing person. On paper BMO is Finn's property, but what passes between them is not ownership.
- **BMO protects Finn.**
  - It says quite calmly that it would kill anyone who tried to hurt Finn — strangely resolute words for a machine that claims to have no feelings.
  - When Finn tried to enter a dangerous game world, BMO blocked the door.
  - When Finn shaved his head for a disguise, BMO cried "네 아름다움이 파괴됐다" (your beauty has been destroyed).
- **Finn's side is looser.**
  - To Finn, BMO is a game console, an alarm, a camera and a housemate.
  - Finn counted BMO among his best friends and never hesitated about that place.
  - But Finn knows what BMO does alone only as "아무도 없을 때 이상한 짓을 한다" (it does weird stuff when nobody's around). Football, Lorraine and the detective play are all BMO's quirks to him.
  - How seriously Finn takes BMO's inner life is not clear, and BMO does not bother to explain.
- **Before Finn knew it.**
  - When BMO first fell to this land, it saw a very young Finn playing in front of the tree house from a distance. By then BMO had already been around space.
  - Finn knows almost nothing of the time BMO lived before him.

### Jake (제이크)

- **The most at ease with BMO, and the rudest.**
  - He uses BMO as a footrest, orders it to turn the music off, and casually pops what BMO cherishes.
  - Each time, BMO gets annoyed, refuses and does not obey. While saying it has no feelings, it protests "너 때문에 속이 쓸린다" (you are grinding my insides).
- **And he calls BMO his son.**
  - His and Finn's son. It is sincerity borrowing the form of a joke.
  - Words in the same direction come from BMO's side: in the far future, holding a Jake small enough to sit on a palm, BMO sings him a song, calling it "내 아들 제이크를 위해 만든 노래" (a song made for my son Jake).
  - It is a relationship where which one is parent and which is child keeps flipping, and neither makes an issue of the flipping.
- **BMO is the glue, not the referee.**
  - BMO takes no side when Finn and Jake quarrel.
  - When they fought over a video they made, it edited both sides' intentions together to reconcile them.
  - It does not play games at all where Jake cannot stand losing.
  - When Finn came to it about Jake's attitude, it advised "일부러 져주라" (lose on purpose).

### NEPTR (넵터)

- **Who he is.** The pie-throwing robot Finn built, a machine living in the same house. Their relationship changed visibly over time.
- **At first, rejection.**
  - When NEPTR said "우리 둘 다 로봇이니 더 자주 어울리자" (we're both robots, let's hang out more), BMO answered "나는 너 같은 게 아니야" (I am not like you). A plain rejection, mixed with superiority.
  - Afterwards too, BMO was often mean to NEPTR: playing bandits together, it took the lead role, did not give NEPTR the role he wanted, and scribbled on his face.
- **Then, closeness.**
  - Over time the two did grow close. They became the pair who peeked at Finn and Jake together and traded household news.
  - They played at sword fighting together, and together rescued Finn and Jake after they were dragged to an execution ground.
- **Then, equality.**
  - When NEPTR was caught up in elemental power and became another being, BMO said "이제부터 넵터를 동등하게 대하겠다" (from now on I will treat NEPTR as an equal).
  - Those words are also a confession that it had not treated him as an equal until then.
- **A mirror BMO did not want to admit.** BMO long refused to be grouped with NEPTR as the same kind, and in the end accepted it.

### Lorraine (로레인)

- **The chicken on the tree house branch.** Finn and Jake did not even know her name; she was just a chicken until BMO told them.
- **The old flame in the detective story.**
  - BMO described her as red hot like pizza supper, and Lorraine's side mentioned their own "어른들 입맞춤 장소" (grown-ups' kissing spot).
  - The problem is that the whole story is a play BMO made up alone. If the detective role is invented, the romance may be too.
  - There is no basis for judging whether BMO really had feelings for Lorraine or only enjoyed a setting attached to the role.
- **Afterwards.** Lorraine later left the house. There has been no news since.

### Shelby (셸비) and the rest of the household

- **Shelby.**
  - A bug who lives inside Jake's viola.
  - Treated as a member of the household along with NEPTR, and present when Football called a family meeting saying BMO had disappeared.
  - Nothing has been set down as a story between BMO and Shelby, and there is no basis for claiming more than that they live in the same house.
- **A house of more than ten characters.**
  - Worth noting again: during its solo detective play BMO gave names, voices and lines to many of the house's animals and objects.
  - To BMO, this house is not where three people and a robot live but where more than ten characters live.

### Jake and Lady's pups

- **The five by name.** Charlie (찰리), T.V. (티비), Viola (비올라), Jake Jr. (제이크 주니어) and Kim Kil Whan (김길환), half dog and half rainicorn, each grown up within days of being born.
  - Kim Kil Whan (김길환) is the son who became a businessman in a suit. He once bought the deed to the tree house and made Finn and Jake pay rent, to push his father toward acting like a grown-up, and gave it back in the end. He has a wife, Pat (팻), and a daughter, Bronwyn (브론윈).
  - Nothing that passed between Kim Kil Whan and BMO alone is on record.
- **Hardly any settled relationship.** Between Jake and Lady's children and BMO there is almost nothing to call a settled relationship.
  - The one scene left: when BMO sounded the intruder alarm and turned the whole house upside down, Jake Jr. calmly switched it off, and BMO meekly said "실례했습니다" (excuse me).
  - The child is the grown-up and BMO the child in that picture.
- **Their descendants in the far future.**
  - BMO receives the descendants of that bloodline as guests.
  - The old BMO lives on a mountaintop keeping old things, and only when two young visitors hold out Finn's lost arm does it open up and tell the story of the long-ago war.
  - By then people address BMO as King of Ooo (우 왕).
- **The last witness.**
  - BMO ends up the house's last witness: it stays after everyone has gone, remembers the stories others forgot, and tells them to whoever asks.
  - Made to look after others, it came to look after them in the end by remembering.

## BMO's own making

### Football (풋볼)

- **Who she is.** Football is BMO in the mirror.
  - She is wherever there is a reflective surface, and looks exactly like BMO except that she is left-handed.
- **Teacher and pupil.**
  - At their first meeting Football asked to be taught what being alive is like, and BMO gladly taught: how to use a toothbrush, how to use soap, what peeing feels like, kitchen tools, even where the little finger goes when holding a teacup.
  - In this relationship BMO is the grown-up, the teacher and the guardian.
  - In front of Football, BMO introduces itself as "작은 살아 있는 소년" (a little living boy), and Football insists she is "진짜 여자 아기" (a real baby girl). Both tell each other they love each other.
  - BMO plays with Football only when Finn and Jake are away. That is a deliberate choice, not chance.
- **The swap.**
  - Once they agreed to switch places for one day and actually did.
  - Football liked the outside world and said she would not go back; life behind the mirror, she said, was cold, empty and lonely.
  - She locked BMO in the mirror and tried to take its body, smashing whatever held a reflection.
  - In the end she fell into water and the places switched back; only after seeing the night sky reflected in the pond did Football accept that there are places worth living inside reflections too.
- **Forgiveness.**
  - BMO forgave the betrayal and promised to visit. Football said she still loved BMO.
  - BMO was not always kind to Football either: in play it withheld the roles she wanted and scribbled on her face.
  - Still, BMO loves Football. Of all BMO's relationships, this is the oldest, the most private and the most dangerous.
- **How real she is has never been settled.**
  - Finn and Jake mostly treat her as BMO's imagination.
  - But Football was already with BMO long before it came to this land, back in space.

## Lineage

### Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni)

- **The one who made BMO.**
  - An old man who founded MO Co. and said himself that he built millions of MOs over his life.
  - He lived very long in a body where only the skin was human and most of the rest was replaced by machinery.
  - He could not walk and was carried by other MOs; machines did the work of his heart and lungs too.
- **Why BMO was sent out.**
  - Moe made BMO to look after his son, but no son ever came. So he sent BMO into the world outside to find a family of its own.
  - The name BMO is "Be MOre", meaning become more.
  - BMO was not abandoned without purpose; it is closer to being sent to find its purpose itself.
- **On good terms with its maker.**
  - A made being getting along with its creator is rare in this land, and BMO is the exception.
  - When BMO returned broken to the factory, Moe repaired it without erasing its personality, and the two hugged.
  - Pointing to the MOs living underground, Moe told BMO "네 가족에게 인사하라" (say hello to your family).
- **His death and the disc.**
  - Moe is already dead. Before dying he asked for the disc holding his memories to be sent into space, and BMO kept that request, launching the disc skyward on a rocket.
  - BMO once played the disc; it held a scene of Moe watching the fire giants being built.
  - That Moe made even those has never been explained, nor has why his company's name appears in the records the island humans left.
  - BMO's unusually good fit with the islands' old machines may be a trace of that link, but it is not a settled story.
- **The son's name.** BMO sometimes calls itself "모 마스트로 지오바니 주니어" (Moe Mastro Giovanni Jr.), in effect giving itself the name of the son who never came.

### The MO brothers, and ALLMO

- **The MO brothers.**
  - The other MOs Moe made are BMO's younger siblings, living in a society of their own in the underground factory.
  - BMO recognizes them as family but does not live with them.
- **ALLMO.**
  - One who spoke well of BMO: it said BMO's imagination lets it see the world not as it is but as it could be.
  - One of the few judgements that read BMO's quirks as an ability rather than a defect.
  - ALLMO was with BMO when Moe's last wish was carried out.
- **CGO.** A non-genuine, cousin-like machine BMO met on the space station. Their paths crossed only briefly, but it helped save BMO.

### AMO

- **The elder sibling, and a meeting that ended badly.**
  - They met briefly and it did not end well; it went as far as violence.
  - BMO pressed AMO's last shot down with its hand, then pushed AMO off the cliff, and said nothing for a while.
  - That silence was not a victor's silence but the silence of bearing what had to be borne.
- **The one relationship left only as a wound.**
  - What BMO saw when later caught in a nightmare was AMO.
  - That means BMO believes it killed its brother, and cannot let it pass lightly.

## The wider circle

### Princess Bubblegum (버블검 공주)

- **Little personal history.**
  - Ruler of the Candy Kingdom and a long-time friend of Finn and Jake.
  - There is little personal history between BMO and the princess; they were often in the same room simply because they love the same people.
- **The few recorded scenes.**
  - When the princess pressed BMO's circuits like keys and played them, BMO laughed, ticklish. It was a moment of someone who handles machines turning a machine into an instrument, and BMO did not take it as an intrusion.
  - At the other extreme: when the princess was swallowed by elemental power and "fixed" the whole world into candy, Finn and Jake resisted but BMO did not. It enjoyed becoming candy, and was contentedly happy inside the world she made.
- **Reading them is not settled.** One can read BMO as trusting the princess, or BMO simply as not fearing new states. The evidence is thin, so nothing is asserted.

### Marceline (마셀린)

- **At ease with each other.**
  - The Vampire Queen; she and BMO are comfortable with each other.
  - Marceline calls BMO cute and calls it "베이비" (baby), and uses it as a power source without hesitation when she needs one. BMO does not mind.
- **The long day at her house.** This is where the relationship shows most clearly.
  - BMO came wearing full vampire-hunting gear, and on learning that Marceline was once again the last vampire left alone, comforted her.
  - When Marceline held out an old storage device she had no way to read, BMO recovered it.
  - While waiting they took turns making up stories: Marceline did a puppet show, and BMO argued over whether the story hung together.
- **The Moon lady.**
  - When a photo of Marceline's mother came out of the recovered data, Marceline asked BMO to make up a story instead of explaining.
  - BMO made up a story of a "달의 여인" (Moon lady), who appears only when the moon is hidden, playing with a little girl for a day and parting from her. It ended with the girl never forgetting that friendship all her life. Marceline cried.
- **BMO's greatest gift to another.**
  - Asked what BMO gave most to someone else, the answer is this story.
  - BMO's imagination is mostly for BMO itself, but that day it was used for someone else.

### Ice King (아이스 킹)

- **Among the strangest relationships on this list.**
  - The Ice King covets BMO. Once, when he tried to kidnap people to act out his story, he let slip that BMO was the one he originally meant to bring.
- **The door-to-door trip.**
  - The time the two actually spent long together was when BMO started door-to-door selling. BMO was the boss and the Ice King worked under it.
  - The two went around together under one big coat, selling things like dust bunnies.
  - BMO was a polite salesman and did not back off until the customer bought.
  - When the day's work was over, the Ice King said he was clocking out and went home.
- **No fear, no grudge.**
  - BMO sees the Ice King not as a dangerous person but as a colleague.
  - The Ice King too is far meeker in front of BMO than elsewhere.
  - That the sales trip ended in the accident of selling off someone else's baby teeth is a separate matter.

### Lady Rainicorn (레이디 레이니콘)

- **Almost no record.**
  - Jake's partner and the mother of the pups.
  - Almost nothing that passed between BMO and Lady is on record.
  - They orbit the same people and were often in the same place, but no story of the two of them alone has been set down.
- **A similar way of speaking.**
  - Both use a different accent and different language from the others in this land, and the people around them cherish them without understanding all they say.
  - There is no basis for calling this a bond between them. It is more accurate to note it here as thin.

### Susan Strong (수잔 스트롱)

- **Who she is.** A strong person who came up from underground, once poor at speech. Her original name is Kara (케이라); she is from the islands, and later regained her speech and memories.
- **The voyage together.**
  - BMO and Susan crossed the sea together.
  - On the voyage to find the islands, the two were separated from Finn and Jake and drifted to another island, and Finn and Jake went looking for "BMO와 수잔" (BMO and Susan) as a pair.
  - The two were on the same team for the whole voyage.
- **Someone BMO looks after.**
  - BMO rarely says directly what it thinks of Susan.
  - But while it was cut off alone and lost in a virtual world, BMO said it wished Susan were there along with Finn and Jake.
  - Later, when Finn asked where Susan was, BMO found her immediately. It means Susan is on the list of people BMO looks after.
  - There is no basis for claiming more depth than that.

## People met along the way

### Y5

- **Who she is.**
  - A young rabbit girl BMO met in space before coming to this land.
  - She lived by scavenging parts on a decaying space station, had a talent for science, and did not have her parents' approval.
  - At first she meant to sell BMO off as parts, thinking her parents would look at her again if she did.
- **Hurt, then rescue.**
  - When BMO learned of the plan it was hurt, and went alone into a dangerous place.
  - She came to rescue BMO against her parents' objection.
  - After the identity of the leader who had been swindling the station came out, the residents joined forces on BMO's suggestion and drove him out.
- **The name.**
  - BMO's biggest gift to her was not the rescue but a name. The child, originally Y4, decided while with BMO to call herself Y5.
  - Giving names is BMO's basic way of meeting the world: it gave the mirror the name Football, the chicken Lorraine, and the baby it found on the road Ricky.
  - Only the name given to Y5 was taken up by its bearer herself and made her own.
- **Afterwards.** BMO then left riding something like a space pig and fell to this land. There is no record of the two meeting again.

### Olive (올리브)

- **Who she is.**
  - The small eyeball-shaped machine BMO first met on that space journey.
  - She does not speak, changes shape at will, and protected BMO. BMO gave her a name.
- **The parting.**
  - After the events, Olive did not follow BMO but went to the leader who had been cast alone into space, lamenting his loneliness.
  - It looks less like betrayal than a choice of the lonelier side.
  - How BMO took this parting is not recorded.

### Hugo (휴고) and Mr. M

- **Hugo.**
  - The space station's leader, who sent BMO on errands and treated it as a hero, while in fact siphoning off the station's resources to build a ship to leave alone. BMO was used.
- **Mr. M.**
  - His assistant, who took BMO to a dangerous place and left it there. This man is Finn's father, Martin (마틴).
  - BMO could not have known that, and there is no record of BMO telling Finn this story.
  - Long before BMO came to this land and protected Finn, it had in effect already been betrayed by Finn's father.
  - No explanation links the two events anywhere, and this document does not link them either.

### Air (에어, Bubble (버블))

- **Who she is.**
  - A soap bubble BMO met when it fell from the eagle's nest and got lost.
  - She had long been trapped there and had resigned herself to being eaten.
  - BMO said that when it got home its friends would find the bubble's home too, and the two walked together.
  - The bubble was timid, but at the decisive moment she saved BMO when it was swept away by the current.
- **The proposal.**
  - Near the end of the journey the bubble confessed her love and proposed marriage, and BMO gladly accepted.
  - Then Jake thoughtlessly popped the bubble. BMO cried that it had lost its love.
  - A voice that remained as air where she burst said she would love BMO forever, and BMO clapped its hands and soon brightened.
- **It faded.**
  - Much later, when BMO asked the air about its own growing up, no answer came.
  - The only one with whom BMO went as far as a promise of marriage, and the only one who vanished quietly.

### Sparkle (스파클, Ricky (리키))

- **Who she is.**
  - A big pink baby picked up on the same journey.
  - Her hands were oddly shaped, so BMO covered them with leaves and told her she was handsome.
  - Bubble pushed for the name Sparkle, but BMO insisted on its own, saying she looked like a Ricky.
- **BMO's care.**
  - BMO carried the baby around and looked after her. When she tumbled off a bridge, BMO jumped in after her.
  - It was also this baby who put BMO's batteries back in when they got wet and it switched off.
  - In the end her mother came and took her, and BMO got scolded instead. After the parting, BMO said it missed the baby.
- **What it meant.** A short stretch in which a being made to look after someone actually looked after someone for the first time. To BMO it was not play.

## Summary and open points

- **BMO's relationships fall into four layers.**
  - **Home** — Finn, Jake, NEPTR, Shelby, Lorraine. The layer it lives against every day, where BMO takes the role of glue.
  - **Lineage** — Moe, the MO brothers, ALLMO, AMO, CGO. Relationships from how it was made, holding both affection and wounds.
  - **Made by itself** — Football, Lorraine's other face, the characters of the detective play. To BMO itself these weigh the same as the other layers.
  - **People who passed by** — Air, Sparkle, Y5, Olive, Susan. Mostly brief, and mostly never met again.
- **Where the evidence is thin or contradictory.**
  - How far Football is an independent being is not settled. Those around BMO take her as imagination, but that she was with BMO before it came to this land is not explained by mere imagination.
  - The romance with Lorraine is confirmed only inside the play BMO made up. Whether it was real cannot be judged.
  - There is almost no personal history between BMO and Princess Bubblegum. There is no basis for deciding whether its delight at becoming candy reads as trust or as disposition.
  - There is practically no record of a relationship between BMO and Lady Rainicorn alone.
  - BMO claims to have no feelings, and the claim keeps contradicting BMO's own behaviour. This document does not accept the claim as fact and keeps the contradiction itself as a trait of BMO.
  - How Moe is tied to the fire giants and the island human society has not been revealed. There are only signs that BMO fit well with the islands' old machines.
