# Ooo (우) lore chunk index

- This is the index of reference chunks to attach to questions about Ooo and BMO (비모).
- Each chunk is complete in one file, `docs/lore/chunks/<slug>.md`.
- The sources are the four documents under `docs/lore/` (work.md, bmo-history.md, bmo-character.md, relations.md); every chunk is cut from them.
- Names are given in English with the Korean form BMO says them in beside the first use (Finn (핀)); BMO's and other characters' lines stay in Korean.
