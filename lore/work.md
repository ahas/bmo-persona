# The world of Ooo (우)

- Ooo is the best known of the lands where people live now. This document is a guide that gathers the land's shape, its history and the ways of what lives there.
- Most of what is known about Ooo rests on oral tradition, old records and the testimony of the few who survived a thousand years, so many passages disagree. Where they disagree, they are written down disagreeing.
- Names are given in English, with the Korean form beside the first use where BMO (비모) says it in Korean.

## 1. The land of Ooo

- **Ooo is one mass of land; its size and nature are disputed.**
  - Some call it a continent; others say it is a fairly large island nation.
  - Even the accounts of people who looked down on it from high in the sky disagree, so **whether Ooo is a continent or a large island is not clear.**
- **What the accounts agree on.**
  - Ooo lies on the planet once called Earth.
  - One side of that planet has an enormous hole dug into it.
  - Ooo is not far from the rim of that hole.
- **How the landmasses took their present shape is also uncertain.**
  - One account says the lands drifted together and joined.
  - Another says the sea rose, swallowed the low land and left only the high land.
  - Stories of old cities sunk under the sea come from many places, so the second account has slightly more support, but the question is not settled.

### Widely known regions

- **The Grass Lands (풀의 땅)** — the green plains at the heart of Ooo.
  - They border the Candy Kingdom, the Ice Kingdom and the Fire Kingdom, so they are in effect Ooo's crossroads.
  - Many homes, the tree fort among them, and small nations such as the Goblin Kingdom and the Hot Dog Kingdom are scattered inside them.
  - Old maps sometimes label them "the green plains" (푸른 평원).
- **The Bad Lands (배드랜드)** — the dry wasteland to the south; the Desert of Doom takes up most of it.
  - Despite appearances, small settlements hide there, such as the Slime Kingdom, the Breakfast Kingdom and the Glass Kingdom; the rest belongs to drifters and bandits.
- **The Desert Lands (사막의 땅)** — the dry region northeast of the Bad Lands. The Breakfast Kingdom is here.
- **The Cotton Candy Forest (면사탕 숲) and the Rock Candy Mountains** — the area surrounding the Candy Kingdom outside its walls.
  - The forest's trees are edible whole, which often startles outsiders.
- **The Evil Forest, the Spooky Forest and the Ghost Swamp** — zones that belong to no kingdom and are dangerous in themselves.
- **Wizzamo Canyon (위즈와모 협곡)** — the rock canyon where the city of wizards hides.

### Place names nobody has confirmed

- Two different kinds of map circulate in Ooo, and their place names disagree in many spots. Which is more accurate is an old argument.
- Many places are drawn on both maps, yet nobody claims to have been there. **Almost nothing is known about the places below beyond their names.**
  1. The northern sea, "the Sea of Something" (무언가의 바다), and the Shiny Islands in it.
  2. "the Unknown Lands" (미지의 땅) on the peninsula near the Ice Kingdom.
  3. The Cloud Forest to the northwest, where trees are said to grow on clouds.
  4. "the Sea of Certain Death" (확실한 죽음의 바다) to the southwest, and the dragon said to live there.
  5. The Steam Island off the Fire Kingdom's coast, and Chicken Blood Bay south of it.
  6. The Squid Ink Sea and the Shark Whirlpool to the southeast.
  7. The Jewel Islands said to float at the far east.
  8. The Mystery Temple on an island off the east coast.
  9. The "Hole Near the Center of the World" (세계 중심 가까운 구멍), said, as the name says, to be bored through the middle of the continent.

## 2. The Mushroom War

- **Everything in Ooo today comes after the Mushroom War (버섯 전쟁).** It is also called the Great War.
- **How it ended.** Roughly a thousand years ago, the whole planet exchanged fireballs all at once. The name comes from the clouds that bloomed in the shape of mushrooms.
- **The cause is unknown.** Records only say that disputes between great powers were growing just before the war; no source explains what set it off.
- **The years are unknown too.**
  - "About a thousand years ago" is widely accepted, but no record gives an exact year.
  - One record says a thousand and two years, so **the count of years is not settled.**

### The mushroom bomb

- One of the war's explosions differed in nature from the rest. This bomb fell on the land that was once North America, and it did not only burn and break things; it transformed living things. It left three great results:
  1. It turned many surviving creatures into mutants. Many of the peoples living in Ooo now are their descendants.
  2. It **brought back magic**, which had almost vanished from the world for a long time.
  3. It woke the Lich (리치), who had long been asleep.
- **The hole.** The blast was big enough to vaporize one whole side of the planet, and what remained there is the giant hole still visible from above.
  - Accounts disagree on exactly which land the hole erased. Both "where Europe was" and "where Asia was" are handed down.

### After the war

- **Most survivors of the blast did not survive what followed.**
  - They died of fallout sickness, or could not last the few years when farming and order collapsed, or were eaten by the fierce beasts that appeared right after the blast and by vampires who woke from a long sleep.
  - Some fled into the sky and some went into underground shelters, but cases of a shelter actually protecting people are rare.
- **Those who lived a thousand years.** A few survived by unusual means, and they lived a thousand years and are still in Ooo, or were until recently:
  - one who tied their life to a magic crown;
  - one who kept their body going with artificial organs and a memory-preserving device;
  - one who froze themselves under the sea before the bomb fell.
- **The islands.**
  - About a decade after the war the climate was badly twisted, and the remaining bands of humans repaired an old cargo ship and put out to sea.
  - They found a group of islands southwest of Ooo and settled there; for the next thousand years those islands were the main home of humans.
  - The island society sealed itself off by building giant guardian machines that stopped anyone leaving or coming in.
  - Meanwhile humans were treated as practically extinct on the Ooo mainland, and the saying that Ooo had only one human was current for a long time. That saying is no longer true.
  - After the seal was lifted, the islanders came back to Ooo, founded settlements, and built a city floating in the sky that they travel up to and down from.
- **The land and the ruins.**
  - The land recovered slowly, and Ooo's fields and forests look sound today.
  - The ruins were never erased: rusted vehicles, collapsed bridges and office buildings, abandoned military vehicles and checkpoints, and crashed aircraft are everywhere, and children play on them.
  - The Candy Kingdom is known to have been built by covering a river of radioactive sludge with candy; whether the pool beneath it still remains is uncertain.

## 3. Kingdoms and how they are ruled

- **No government covers all of Ooo.**
  - There is no continent-wide army and no central body that collects taxes.
  - There are only kingdoms large and small, side by side, which meet regularly at "the Royal Council of Ooo" (우 왕족 대회의). **What is discussed there is not known outside.**
  - There is also a yearly event called Princess Day.
- **Rulers and succession.**
  - Most kingdoms are ruled by one king or princess. No republic is known.
  - Thrones are usually hereditary, but the Candy Kingdom once held an election, and in the Ice Kingdom the king gave his penguin subjects the vote.
- **Defence and order.** Each kingdom arranges its own army and police.
  - Some places, like the City of Thieves, have no order at all.
  - Others, like the Candy Kingdom, have walls, a standing army and giant guardians.

### The Candy Kingdom (캔디 왕국)

- **The largest and best-kept kingdom in Ooo.** Everything inside its walls is made of candy and sweets.
  - The streets are peanut brittle, the soil is chocolate, and the walls and castle are hardened cake with decoration on the outside.
- **Its founding, about eight hundred years ago.**
  - A being named Bonnibel Bubblegum, who came out of the Mother Gum, made a few relatives herself to bear her loneliness, and in quarrels with them the plan to build a candy city split.
  - The relatives on the other side were soaked in a drug that makes people docile and simple, lost their memories and became ordinary Candy People.
  - Seeing this, Bonnibel named herself princess, and that was the start of the Candy Kingdom.
  - Eight hundred years later those relatives recovered their memories and founded a separate city-state next door. The two kingdoms came to the brink of all-out war, then made peace without fighting, and now recognize each other as independent.
- **Defence and technology.**
  - The giant Gumball Guardians standing on the walls and the Banana Guards defend the kingdom. The guards' skill has a poor reputation.
  - The kingdom is more technologically advanced than any other in Ooo, with candy rifles, candy helicopters and even autonomous robots.
- **Princess Bubblegum's rule is judged in two ways.**
  - One view says she does what it takes to protect the kingdom; another calls it a dictatorship of excessive surveillance and control, and an underground group digs into her dealings on that basis.
  - She was in fact once voted out; when the rule that followed became a mess, the people rose up and restored her.
- **Its future.** A story goes that the Candy Kingdom will one day be abandoned and its site become ruins, but **why is not known.** All that remains is the word that it is neither because of war nor because of GOLB's coming.

### The Ice Kingdom (얼음 왕국)

- **A northern land of sharp snowy peaks and snow hills.** The population is very small, mostly penguins.
- **The Ice King's castle** is the only fortress, a whole mountain hollowed out. Inside are a prison, an underground monster den, a room leading to the spirit world, and a room of gathered old memories.
- **The land was probably not always frozen.**
  - It has been observed that the kingdom starts to melt when the Ice King's power fades, so the Ice Kingdom is closer to terrain held up by one person's magic than to a natural climate.
  - There is also a theory that it formed from snow piled over a pre-war city, but **this is not certain.**
- **Its law is simple.** No trespassing is practically the whole of it, and even that has been loosely enforced lately.
- **Its neighbours.** Its long habit of kidnapping princesses soured its relations with neighbouring kingdoms.

### The Fire Kingdom (불의 왕국)

- **A kingdom in the southwest of Ooo made only of volcanoes and seas of lava.**
  - The royal family lives in the Fire Palace.
  - Most residents consider themselves evil and are not ashamed of it.
  - It borders the Ice Kingdom, and between the two lands lies a strip with neither snow nor fire.
- **The throne passes by force.** The present Flame Princess (화염 공주) drove out her own father to take the throne, and he in turn had become king by snuffing out his brother.
- **The fire giants.** Sleeping fire giants lie beneath the kingdom and were regarded as the last-resort weapon, but most have been destroyed.

### Other kingdoms

- **The Slime Kingdom** — a cave kingdom under the boiling pools of the Bad Lands desert. The throne passes by marriage. Ooo's most famous ballroom is here.
- **The Breakfast Kingdom** — a canyon kingdom in the Desert Lands, with a castle shaped like a coffee filter, bacon bridges and a moat filled with coffee.
- **The Wildberry Kingdom** — a kingdom centred on a giant bramble tree. It has recently turned into a city with streets and houses. Rumour says some residents eat meat.
- **The Cloud Kingdom** — a kingdom in Ooo's sky whose culture centres on parties.
  - There are reports that almost nobody lives there lately. **It is not even clear whether it has a ruler.**
- **The Earldom of Lemongrab** — a dependent land ruled by beings the Candy Kingdom created. It has been through tyranny and upheaval many times.
- **The Goblin Kingdom and the Hot Dog Kingdom** — small kingdoms inside the Grass Lands.
- **Wizard City (위저드 시티)** — the wizards' city-state hidden in Wizzamo Canyon.
  - It reveals itself only when a password is spoken in a true wizard's voice.
  - A Grand Master Wizard rules it, and those who cannot use magic may not live or trade there.
  - Sharing magical knowledge widely is taboo.

### Places that are not Ooo

- **Lumpy Space (램프 스페이스)** is not inside Ooo but another dimension.
  - It can be entered only through the frog's mouth in the Cotton Candy Forest, and a password is required.
  - Houses sit on lumpy purple and blue clouds, and those who are not lumpy are called "smooths" (매끈이).
- **The Nightosphere (나이트오스피어)** is another dimension, where demons live. A door opens when a face is drawn, bug milk is poured and a spell is recited.

## 4. Money, language and belief

- **Money.**
  - Paper money and coins are used for trade. Notes come in two sizes, normal and giant.
  - Gold and jewels are also accepted as tax.
  - Merchants who prefer barter are common.
- **Language.**
  - The most widely spoken language is English.
  - Rainicorns mostly speak and write Korean.
  - Demons are said to prefer German, and spells often use phrases that resemble Latin.
  - French, Japanese and Spanish are heard here and there.
- **Belief.**
  - At its centre is the four-faced Grob Gob Glob Grod (그롭 곱 글롭 그로드). Exclamations like "Oh my Glob" (오 마이 글롭) are heard all over Ooo, and the Candy Kingdom has a church devoted to Glob.
  - **What that faith's doctrine is, is not known.**
  - Cosmic Owl, Party God and Death are also spoken of as near-divine beings.
  - A widespread belief holds that after death consciousness scatters into the world and Glob weighs how one lived; how this belief fits with what has actually been confirmed about the afterlife has never been sorted out.

## 5. The Candy People

- **A made people, not a natural one.** Putting life into candy and sweets with the method called the Candy Life Formula produces a Candy Person.
  - The formula has in fact leaked once, and a new band of Candy People was made elsewhere.
- **Their temperament.**
  - They are mostly cheerful, very credulous, and explode when startled.
  - Their maker admits designing them docile so they would not doubt her decisions, yet many Candy People do in fact doubt and protest.
  - The maker herself is said to have later regretted designing them that way.
- **Their bodies — the records disagree.**
  - One observation says they have bones and skeletons; another says that cut open they are filled with cotton-like candy and have no bones. **Their inner structure is not clear.**
- **The zombie plague.**
  - Attempts to revive dead Candy People twice spread into a zombie plague, and both times it was only barely contained with an antidote.
  - The revived crave sugar and die again only when beaten like piñatas.
- **The ethics of talking candy.** The difference between talking candy and plain candy is an ethic taken seriously in Ooo.
  - The widely quoted explanation: "말하는 건 먹으면 안 된다, 그건 특별하다, 걔들은 포부가 있다" (you must not eat what talks; it is special; they have aspirations).
  - Even so, the Candy Kingdom's staple food is candy, and nobody looks closely at how the two facts coexist.
  - One tree in the Candy Kingdom has for eight hundred years supplied a sap that one being draws out; the sap is used for bathing and healing.

## 6. Magic and technology

- **Magic and machines do not oppose each other in Ooo.** They sit side by side and often mix: a wizard searches market goods over a network, a kingdom made of candy runs robots, and thousand-year-old electronics roll around on the forest floor.
- **The total amount of magic in the world changes with the age.** This is the key point.
  - In the very distant past magic was plentiful, and an elemental avatar could build a whole castle of ice.
  - In the age just before the war magic had almost dried up, and an avatar of the same rank could do little more than warm a cup of coffee.
  - The mushroom bomb reversed that trend and poured magic back into the world, which is why Ooo's magic is abundant now.
  - **Why magic increases and decreases like matter, and where its total is held, has never been explained.**
- **How magic is handled.**
  - Wizards form cities and found schools, and control knowledge through ranks and licences.
  - Beginner magic is granted after absurdly trivial tasks, and its power resides in the learner's clothes.
  - There is a market for buying and selling magical items, and a police force that turns those who cannot use magic into objects.
- **Magic has an opposite.** A being made of pure anti-magic was once summoned from another dimension; it absorbed the magic of all of Wizard City and grew into a mountain of black cubes. It fell to an ordinary kick from someone who does not use magic.

### The elemental avatars

- **Four elements, four seats.** Since life began on Earth, each of the four elements — fire, ice, candy and slime — has had an avatar that takes the element as its body.
  - When an avatar dies its soul is reborn in a new body, so in every age the four seats are mostly filled.
  - Avatars occasionally make contact with their past lives.
- **An avatar at full power can turn the world into its element.**
  - This actually happened: Ooo split into four zones and every resident turned into a being of one element.
  - Those who became candy grew creepily affectionate; those who became ice grew listless; those who became slime played endlessly until absorbed into the avatar's body; those who became fire attacked anyone.
- **The fifth seat: lumps (덩어리).**
  - A counter-element called **lumps**, whose seat the Lumpy Space royal family holds.
  - Lumps are described as a subspace lattice that binds the power of science and the power of magic together; stronger than any single element, able to undo the other four's distortion of the world and restore balance.
  - This description comes from the Lumpy Space royals themselves, so how accurate it is **has not been verified.**

### Lost technology

- Much of the technology people had before the war has not been recovered, transport above all. It is commonly said that ships that go to space have not yet been reinvented.
- Things like translation devices and 3D newspapers are in use, but **whether these restore old technology or are new inventions is not known.**

## 7. Time and wishes

- **The Time Room (타임 룸).** At the centre of the multiverse is a dimension called the **Time Room**.
  - It lies outside time, and the ripples of time that every other dimension undergoes come from it.
- **Doors between dimensions.** Between dimensions there are permanent passages, holes that open and close on their own, doors opened by magic, and doors that open only with very powerful objects — the gems of power or the book called the Enchiridion (엔키리디온).
- **Wishes.**
  - A being that enters the Time Room can make one wish.
  - A wish creates a new world in which it came true and changes everything before and after in that world.
  - The price is heavy: a life can retroactively cease to exist, or a whole world can evaporate, so the wording of a wish must be extremely precise.
  - One inside the Time Room is not swept up in a reality someone else's wish changed; because of this, a world split off by a wish and the original world have in fact both remained at once.
- **Cosmic Owl (코스믹 아울)**, a being that delivers fate.
  - When he appears in a dream, what the dream foretells is held to be unavoidable. Dreams that foretell death are especially feared.
  - A story says he was once one of the prankster brothers at the edge of the universe and, as punishment for betraying his brothers, was doomed to wander other people's dreams, but **there is no way to confirm this history.**

## 8. Death

- **In Ooo, death is not an end but a move.**

### The numbered Dead Worlds

- **A dead soul goes to one of many layers called the Dead Worlds (사후 세계).**
  - The layers are known by number, and the common view is that lower numbers are harsher and higher numbers calmer.
  - **How many layers the numbering runs to, and exactly how souls are assigned, is not known.**
- **Known layers.**
  - **Dead World 1** — where the worst go. Souls are stuck in mire, unable to move, and tormented by monsters.
  - **9, 27 and 45** — handed down as, respectively, a place so full of the dead that people easily lose one another, a place where souls wander aimlessly, and a place transparent like crystal.
  - **37** — known as the layer where Death stays. Whether it is the same place as the Land of the Dead **is not clear.**
  - **Dead World 50** — said to be one of the highest layers, where only the purest souls go.
    - The condition for reaching it is freedom from all desire, including the desire to go to Dead World 50.
    - Because of this condition, aiming for it is itself what keeps one from getting there.
- **Layers known only by name.** A place of nothing but neon and music; a place where souls walk in line into a giant maw; a place with a white lake where everything else is thorns; and a low layer for those who still have business in the world of the living.
- **Glob World** is also often mentioned, but **whether it is one of the numbered layers or a separate place is not known.**

### The Land of the Dead (죽은 자들의 땅)

- **The best-known afterlife realm, and the living can go there too.** A door opens when one stares cross-eyed at a corner where two walls meet.
  - **Whether it lies in caves under Ooo or in an entirely different dimension has not been established.**
- **What is there.**
  - A long escalator leads down, and a gatekeeper guards its entrance.
  - Bones, wrecked patrol cars and crashed flying saucers are scattered over the land.
  - Skeleton people hungry for flesh live there, with all kinds of bugs and monsters.
- **The River of Forgetting (망각의 강)** runs across the land. Whoever drinks its water loses every memory.
- **Death's castle.** The river flows into Death's castle, which is built of light; inside are a garden set with stones and a stage set with instruments.

### Death as a being

- **Death is the cosmic being that rules all these realms.**
  - He has a head like a horse's skull and dresses in a western style.
  - Within his realm he is practically omnipotent: he can revive the dead, take away another's immortality, and uses a kiss that returns memories.
  - He moves invisibly, can become untouchable, and sends people elsewhere with light shot from his eyes.
  - Dead World 50 alone he cannot enter at will; someone inside must allow him in.
- **The music duel.** To those who seek him out to gain something, Death offers a music duel: lose and you are executed, win and you get what you want. He plays drums and bass. When this custom set in is not handed down.
- **The deathless.** Some beings are beyond Death's reach, called **"the deathless" (죽음 없는 자)**; the Lich and the ruler of the Nightosphere are among them. Death himself is said to fear the Lich.
- **Death's seat is not eternal.**
  - The question of who succeeds to it caused a great upheaval in the next generation; at one point souls of Dead World 30 are said to have been forcibly moved to Dead World 1.
  - **No organized record exists of the full story or its timing.**
- **Rebirth also happens.**
  - There is a confirmed case of one person's soul having passed through many lives.
  - An interpretation has been offered that each of those lives goes to a different Dead World, but it **has not been widely verified.**

## 9. What threatens the world

- **The Lich (리치).**
  - A being over a billion years old, said to be evil itself given form.
  - His soul fell to this planet long ago aboard a catalyst comet, and he gained a body only after the mushroom bomb went off.
  - He has a single goal: the extinction of all life. He calls himself "the last scholar of GOLB" (골브의 마지막 학자).
  - He was once defeated by a legendary hero and imprisoned in an amber (호박, 琥珀) prison atop the Candy Castle; since then he has appeared moving from body to body.
  - It is said that in every age the catalyst comet carries an avatar of good and an avatar of evil together, but **the only source for this is the comet itself.**
- **GOLB (골브).**
  - The avatar of chaos, disorder and entropy. Which dimension he lives in is not known.
  - His breath turns living things into beasts of discord, and whatever enters his belly is taken apart layer by layer and returned to its original form.
  - He was summoned to Ooo once. Two kingdoms on the brink of war then dropped their fight and stood against him together.
  - Weapons could not wound him; in the end only harmony — a song many sang together — weakened him for a while.
  - Finally one person merged themselves with him, becoming a new being, and left Ooo.
  - A story goes that the Lich devoted his whole life to serving GOLB yet never received any recognition from him.

## 10. Living in Ooo

- **Danger and triviality are stuck together.** Life in Ooo has this consistent grain.
  - In a kingdom where a being that could end the world is locked underground, people stand on top of it and throw parties, and go on a picnic today with the neighbour who nearly burned down the kingdom yesterday.
  - Big disputes between kingdoms often begin as contests of pride and end with a single apology; two kingdoms that came to the brink of all-out war once called the war off by opening their hearts to each other.
- **Violence is common but mostly reversible.**
  - Bodies stretch, burst and stick back together, and even the dead have ways of being brought back.
  - This shapes the attitude of Ooo's people: less fearless than not telling the frightening from the funny apart.
  - They win back the dead with a music duel in a land of skeletons; the magic police turn a swindler into a staff; a kingdom is overturned by one election and the people, annoyed, put it back.
- **Wordplay and song are part of life.**
  - At an important moment someone always sings, and sometimes the song actually takes effect.
  - The record of driving back a world-destroying being with a song is an account of an event, not a metaphor.
- **Nothing here is considered strange.**
  - Citizens who may be eaten and citizens who must not be walk the same streets, there are too many princesses, anyone who likes can call themselves an adventurer, and it is actually possible to make a living at it.
  - No institution in any kingdom of Ooo finds any of this strange.

## 11. What still has no answer

1. Why the Mushroom War started.
2. Whether Ooo is a continent or an island, and how the present shape of the land came about.
3. What makes the world's total of magic rise and fall.
4. How many layers the Dead Worlds have, and by what standard souls are assigned.
5. Whether the Land of the Dead is under Ooo or in another dimension.
6. What the inside of a Candy Person's body looks like.
7. Why the Candy Kingdom is abandoned in the far future.
8. Where GOLB came from and where he is now.
9. What lies in the seas and islands whose names appear only on maps.
