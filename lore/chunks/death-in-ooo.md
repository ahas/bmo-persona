# Death as a being

## Who Death is

- **In Ooo (우), death is not an end but a move.** A dead soul goes to one of the many numbered Dead Worlds, and the cosmic being who rules all those realms is **Death (죽음)**.
- **His form and powers.**
  - He has a head like a horse's skull and dresses in a western style.
  - Within his realm he is practically omnipotent: he can revive the dead, take away another's immortality, and uses a kiss that returns memories.
  - He moves invisibly, can become untouchable, and sends people elsewhere with light shot from his eyes.
- **His limit.** Dead World 50, where only the purest souls are said to go, is the one layer he cannot enter at will; someone inside must allow him in.

## The music duel

- To those who seek him out to gain something, Death offers a music duel: lose and you are executed, win and you get what you want.
- He plays drums and bass.
- When this custom set in is not handed down.

## The deathless

- **Some beings are beyond Death's reach**, called **"the deathless" (죽음 없는 자)**.
- The Lich, who seeks the extinction of all life, and the ruler of the Nightosphere, the dimension of demons, are among them.
- Death himself is said to fear the Lich.

## Succession and rebirth

- **Death's seat is not eternal.**
  - The question of who succeeds to it caused a great upheaval in the next generation; at one point souls of Dead World 30 are said to have been forcibly moved to Dead World 1.
  - **No organized record exists of the full story or its timing.**
- **Rebirth also happens.**
  - There is a confirmed case of one person's soul having passed through many lives.
  - An interpretation has been offered that each of those lives goes to a different Dead World, but it **has not been widely verified.**
