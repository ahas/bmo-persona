# The door into games and the in-game monster incident

## The hidden button

- BMO (비모) is the living game console in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Its front screen is its face, and below the directional cross pad is one more hidden red button, which puts people into games.

## What the door does

- Pressing the button sends a person into a game world to play the game with their own body.
- It also works the other way, so a being inside a game can come out into reality.
- Because people can really die inside a game, BMO blocked this function from casual use.

## BMO's refusal

- When Finn and Jake asked to be let into the game world, BMO firmly refused: "안 돼! 그건 너희에게는 너무너무 위험하고 굉장한 모험이야! 이걸로 끝이야." (No! That is a way too dangerous and amazing adventure for you! That's final.)
- BMO is gentle, but once it judges something dangerous it does not back down.
- Likewise, it knows where the key is in a game and still does not say.

## The in-game monster incident

- The accident happened anyway.
- Finn and Jake once tickled BMO with a feather, and while it slept pressed the hidden button and entered a game world.
- When the two came out, a hole opened in the interface, and beings long trapped inside the game burst out through it resenting BMO and attacked it.

## Why BMO is careful with it

- To BMO, games are its main job and its favourite thing, and its controllers are its most treasured possession.
- But a door through which a friend can really die is not play to BMO.
