# What is uncertain in BMO's history

- BMO (비모) is one of the longest-lived machines in the land of Ooo (우), and lived as family in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). Some passages of what BMO went through cannot be asserted.

## The open points

- **When the Drift happened.**
  - What BMO went through on the space station the Drift (드리프트) ends with finding the young Finn and Jake, so it reads naturally as before the tree house years.
  - But the story itself comes down through a collection of records from a much later age, so opinions differ on whether it really belongs to BMO's youth.
  - The content of the events is not disputed; only where they sit is.
- **Who Mr. M is.**
  - Several signs suggest Mr. M (M씨), who betrayed BMO on the Drift, is the same person as Finn's father, Martin Mertens (마틴 머텐스): his looks, his build, his record of drifting through space, the same verbal habit "저 녀석 온다" (here he comes), and his remarks about children who blame their parents.
  - It has never been nailed down as confirmed fact.
- **BMO's age.**
  - Over 1,000 years is the common count, but its basis is unclear.
  - How the fact that BMO's maker, Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni, lived over 1,035 years relates to when BMO was made has not been worked out either.
- **Moe, the fire giants and the islanders.**
  - Moe's memory disc shows the young Moe supervising the construction of the Sleeping Fire Giants (잠자는 불의 거인들), and MO Co. airships also appear in the records of the islanders across the sea.
  - BMO's good fit with island technology also supports the link.
  - But whether Moe made those giants, and what his relationship with the islanders was, has never been explained.
- **How BMO became King of Ooo.**
  - Almost nothing is known of the thousand years after the Gum War (검 전쟁).
  - How BMO lived after Finn and Jake died, why it came to live alone on top of Mount Cragdor, and where the crown came from are all blanks.
- **The relationship with Air.**
  - BMO and Air (에어), the soap bubble, were engaged, and Air said she would be by its side for life.
  - But when BMO later spoke to Air, there was no answer. Whether the relationship continued or ended is not clear.
