# The false Moe on the birthday, and the empty factory

## Background

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크), and keeps its birthday in midwinter, between 24 and 25 December.
- BMO was made by Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni, who founded the MO factory under the Bad Lands.

## The birthday

- That year's birthday, Princess Bubblegum's (버블검 공주) weather machine broke and snow fell on the land of Ooo (우).
- The princess apologized for missing the birthday, but BMO did not mind, because Moe was coming.

## The visitor

- **A large MO knocked at the door.** He said he was Moe, moved into a new body.
- **The mission.** As a birthday present he gave BMO a mission: complete the rite of passage of going to the MO factory and coming back, and it would become a grown-up.
- **The swap.** When BMO said it would miss Finn and Jake, he said he would play "BMO" in its place meanwhile.
- **The walk.** BMO walked through the snow toward the factory, talking about becoming a grown-up to Air (에어), its former fiancée who remained by its side.

## The empty factory

- The factory was empty. DMO, wires sticking out, stood BMO on an "X" mark and opened a garbage chute.
- BMO fell, narrowly dodging saw blades and crushers, until a leg caught and it hung there.
- Barely free, BMO reached a large room holding a giant cube of clumped MO parts: ALLMO.

## Meanwhile at the tree house

- "Moe" passed the time reading newspaper ads.
- When Finn and Jake got bored, he offered Hangman (행맨) as the only game he had.
- The two soon got bored and went out to find and fight a real hangman.
