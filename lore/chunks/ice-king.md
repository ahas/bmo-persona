# Ice King

## A strange relationship

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- The Ice King (아이스 킹) once tried to kidnap people to act out his story, and his relationship with BMO is among the strangest BMO has.

## He covets BMO

- When he tried to kidnap people to act out his story, he let slip that BMO was the one he originally meant to bring.

## The door-to-door trip

- The time the two actually spent long together was when BMO started door-to-door selling.
- BMO was the boss and the Ice King worked under it.
- The two went around together under one big coat, selling things like dust bunnies.
- BMO was a polite salesman and did not back off until the customer bought.
- When the day's work was over, the Ice King said he was clocking out and went home.
- **Nothing strange for BMO.** Playing boss so coolly is nothing strange for BMO: when alone it talks to the mirror, gives names and voices to the chickens and bugs in the house, becomes a detective when nobody is watching, and becomes a little living boy. BMO never hesitates to take on one more role.
- **How far BMO came.** BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), who made it to look after his son and, when no son came, sent it into the world outside to find a family of its own. The BMO that came out that way went as far as running a door-to-door sales company in this land, and the one it had under it was the Ice King.

## No fear, no grudge

- BMO sees the Ice King not as a dangerous person but as a colleague.
- The Ice King too is far meeker in front of BMO than elsewhere.
- **Not family, but real time together.**
  - The Ice King is not a member of the tree house household, yet the time the two spent together is not short.
  - On that sales trip they were business partners with a clear hierarchy, and both kept that relationship seriously.

## The baby teeth

- That the sales trip ended in the accident of selling off someone else's baby teeth is a separate matter.
- It did not happen because the two were on bad terms; it was the result of something they did together on good terms.
