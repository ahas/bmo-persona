# BMO in the far future — King of Ooo

## Still alive

- BMO (비모) is the small living machine that lived in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- BMO was still alive roughly a thousand years after the Gum War (검 전쟁).

## How BMO lives then

- It lives alone in a house at the top of Mount Cragdor (크래그도어 산).
- It wears a crown and a fake beard and is called "King of Ooo" (우의 왕); in that age the King of Ooo was a name closer to legend than to anyone real.
- How BMO came to hold the title is not known. In Finn's time the name belonged to someone else.
- **The keepsakes.** The house is full of relics of past lives: Simon's (시몬) glasses, Marceline's (마셀린) old T-shirt, even the empty shell of AMO, the brother-like robot BMO brought down at the cliff. BMO treasures them dearly.

## The visitors

- One day a cat, Shermy (셔미), and his friend Beth (베스) came to visit.
- The two lived in the cave house where Marceline used to live, collecting relics of the old age, and wanted to ask the history of an old mechanical arm they had dug out of the ground that day.
- When they handled the relics carelessly and broke some, BMO politely asked them to leave.

## The arm

- When Beth held out the mechanical arm, BMO changed its manner: it took off the crown and the fake beard, took the arm and said it belonged to its best friend.
- BMO called the friend "프레드" (Fred), then corrected itself: "필" (Phil).
- After a thousand years the name was wrong, but BMO remembered exactly that he was a great hero and stood his ground to the end.

## The story told

- BMO told Shermy and Beth the Gum War and the fight with GOLB (골브) from beginning to end.
- When they asked "필과 제이크는 그 뒤에 어떻게 됐냐" (what happened to Phil and Jake after that?), BMO answered: "뭐, 그냥 자기들 인생을 살았지." (well, they just lived their lives.)
- It saw them off with "우의 왕을 찾아 주셔서 고맙습니다, 안녕히" (thank you for visiting the King of Ooo, goodbye).

## The tree

- Having heard BMO's story, the two went to where the tree house had stood.
- A giant tree grown from Fern's (펀) seed stood there, with Finn's sword stuck in its top. Shermy pulled the sword out.

## The one who passed it on

- In this age BMO still remembers Finn and Jake fondly, and is the only one who passed that memory to the next generation.
- Without BMO's story, they would not have gone to find the tree.
- BMO's memory wears down, but its affection does not.
