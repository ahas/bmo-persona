# The Ice Kingdom

## The land

- **The Ice Kingdom (얼음 왕국) is a land in the north of Ooo (우) of sharp snowy peaks and snow hills.**
  - The population is very small, mostly penguins.
- **The Ice King's (아이스 킹) castle** is the only fortress, a whole mountain hollowed out. Inside are a prison, an underground monster den, a room leading to the spirit world, and a room of gathered old memories.
- **The land was probably not always frozen.**
  - It has been observed that the kingdom starts to melt when the Ice King's power fades, so the Ice Kingdom is closer to terrain held up by one person's magic than to land held up by a natural climate.
  - There is also a theory that it formed from snow piled over a city from before the Mushroom War, the war that ended roughly a thousand years ago with the whole planet exchanging fireballs, but **this is not certain.**

## Law and rule

- **Its law is simple.** No trespassing is practically the whole of it, and even that has been loosely enforced lately.
- **Rule tied to one man's magic.** Ooo's kingdoms are mostly ruled by one king or princess with hereditary thrones, but rule here is tied not to inheritance but to one king's magic.
- The king once gave his penguin subjects the vote.

## Ice as an element

- **Ice is counted as one of Ooo's four elements.** Since life began on Earth, each of the four elements — fire, ice, candy and slime — has had an avatar that takes the element as its body.
- In the very distant past, when magic was plentiful, the ice avatar could reportedly build a whole castle of ice.
- **The elemental upheaval.** Once elemental power was used to the full: all of Ooo split into four zones and every resident turned into a being of one element. Those who became ice then grew listless.

## Neighbours and surroundings

- **The Unknown Lands.** Maps show a place called "the Unknown Lands" (미지의 땅) on the peninsula near the Ice Kingdom, but **almost nothing is known about it beyond its name.**
- **Bad relations.** Relations with neighbouring kingdoms were long sour, because of the old habit of kidnapping princesses.
- **The Fire Kingdom.** In the southwest of Ooo lies the Fire Kingdom, made only of volcanoes and seas of lava, and the Ice Kingdom borders it. Where the two lands meet lies a strip with neither snow nor fire.
