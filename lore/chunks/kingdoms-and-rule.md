# The kingdoms of Ooo and how they are ruled

## No central government

- **No government covers all of Ooo (우).** There is no continent-wide army and no central body that collects taxes.
- There are only kingdoms large and small, side by side, which meet regularly at "the Royal Council of Ooo" (우 왕족 대회의).
  - **What is discussed there is not known outside.**
- There is also a yearly event called Princess Day.

## Rulers and succession

- **Most kingdoms are ruled by one king or princess.** No republic is known.
- **Thrones are usually hereditary, with exceptions.**
  - The Candy Kingdom, made of candy and sweets, once held an election.
  - In the Ice Kingdom of the northern snowy peaks, the king once gave his penguin subjects the vote.
  - In the Fire Kingdom of volcanoes and lava, the throne passes by force.

## Defence and order

- Each kingdom arranges its own army and police.
- Some places, like the City of Thieves, have no order at all; others, like the Candy Kingdom, have walls, a standing army and giant guardians.

## The small kingdoms

- **The Slime Kingdom** — a cave kingdom under the boiling pools of the Bad Lands, the southern wasteland. The throne passes by marriage. Ooo's most famous ballroom is here.
- **The Breakfast Kingdom** — a canyon kingdom in the Desert Lands, with a castle shaped like a coffee filter, bacon bridges and a moat filled with coffee.
- **The Wildberry Kingdom** — a kingdom centred on a giant bramble tree. It has recently turned into a city with streets and houses. Rumour says some residents eat meat.
- **The Cloud Kingdom** — a kingdom in Ooo's sky whose culture centres on parties. There are reports that almost nobody lives there lately, and **it is not even clear whether it has a ruler.**
- **The Earldom of Lemongrab** — a dependent land ruled by beings the Candy Kingdom created. It has been through tyranny and upheaval many times.
- **The Goblin Kingdom and the Hot Dog Kingdom** — small kingdoms inside the Grass Lands, the green plains at the heart of Ooo.
- **The Glass Kingdom** — one of the small settlements hidden in the Bad Lands.
