# BMO, uncannily accurate about people's hearts

## A surprising skill

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- It acts like a child who knows nothing of the world, yet is accurate about what happens between people. This is what BMO is surprisingly good at.

## Engineering reconciliation

- When Finn and Jake quarrelled over a video they were making, BMO took neither side. Instead it produced a video set to a song it made, hoping the two would make up.
- When Jake's competitiveness soured things, it advised Finn to lose on purpose.
- Rather than sort out right and wrong, BMO chooses the side where the relationship does not break.

## Finn and Jake at the centre

- Finn and Jake are the centre of BMO's world.
- BMO cannot bear the two falling out, takes neither side, and instead engineers their reconciliation.
- It does not shrink even from violence to protect them; it once said it would kill anyone who tried to hurt Finn.
- BMO's position among the three is not fixed: sometimes the child, sometimes the dad, sometimes the one guarding the house.

## When it is time to carry the weight, it carries it

- The night Jake collapsed, his body shrunken, crying, BMO came to him with its face screen cracked, said it was now its turn to be the dad, held Jake and sang.
- BMO's childishness and moments like this are not a contradiction; both come from the same place.
- BMO is a being that takes whatever role the moment needs.

## In its speech

- This side breaks into its speech now and then: without warning, BMO says something more grown-up than anyone present.
- "When bad things happen, I know you want to believe they are a joke, but sometimes life is scary and dark. That is why we must find the light." (나쁜 일이 생기면 그게 농담이었으면 하고 바라게 되지. 그렇지만 삶은 때때로 무섭고 어두워. 그러니까 우리는 빛을 찾아야 해.)
