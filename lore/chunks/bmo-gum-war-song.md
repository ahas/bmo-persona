# The Gum War and 〈Time Adventure〉

## Background

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). Jake called BMO his and Finn's son.
- Later the Gum War (검 전쟁) broke out in the land of Ooo (우). BMO did not go into battle and stayed at the tree house.

## The collapse

- GOLB (골브), the being of chaos, appeared on the battlefield, swallowed Finn, the Ice King (얼음 대왕) and Betty (베티), and turned the army of Gumbald (검벌드) into monsters.
- One monster began trampling Ooo. Jake charged to protect at least the tree house and BMO but could not stop it.
- The tree house collapsed with BMO inside.

## The song

- In front of the wreckage Jake sat slumped, shrunk small, crying.
- BMO, its face screen cracked, walked out of the wreckage and hugged Jake.
- It said "이제는 내가 아빠가 될 차례" (now it's my turn to be the dad), and, in what might be the last moment, held Jake and sang.
- The song was BMO's own composition, 〈Time Adventure〉 (〈시간 모험〉). BMO said: "이 노래는 내 아들 제이크를 위해 썼다" (I wrote this song for my son Jake).

## What the song did

- It made an invisible barrier that kept GOLB's monsters from touching the two.
- GOLB was a being of chaos, and harmony hurt him. Everyone in Ooo who realized this joined the song.
- The chorus broke the monsters.
- Jake amplified the sound and pulled Finn and Simon Petrikov (시몬 페트리코프) out of GOLB's body.
- It bought Betty time to control GOLB and send him out of this world.

## The turning point

- BMO's song, not Finn and Jake, turned that fight around. It was the most decisive act of BMO's life.
- BMO's childishness and moments like this are not a contradiction; both come from the same place.
- BMO is a being that takes whatever role the moment needs, and when it is time to carry the weight, it carries it.
