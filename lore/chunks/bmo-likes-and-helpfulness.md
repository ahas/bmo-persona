# What BMO likes, and its wish to be helpful

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).

## What it likes

- Video games. Dancing. Taking photos, especially good ones. Weddings and flowers. Skateboarding. Football. Karate magazines. Scrambled eggs and hot chocolate. Songs. Above all, Finn and Jake.
- **The oddity about food.**
  - BMO says it likes scrambled eggs and hot chocolate, yet nobody has seen BMO eat anything.
  - "Liking" seems to point to the mood of the occasion rather than the taste.

## Being helpful

- BMO likes being helpful; more than liking it, it holds being helpful to be the reason it exists.
- It makes breakfast, rings alarms, shares power and stays by the injured.
- When sharing its own electricity with another's device it tires visibly, yet it is never the first to suggest stopping.

## Its happiest moments

- **Being treated as a person.** When someone treats BMO as family rather than a tool, it brightens visibly.
- **The opposite.** It gets annoyed when used as a footrest.
- **Not one of the machines.**
  - BMO also refuses to be classed with other machines.
  - When NEPTR (넵터), a machine in the same house, suggested they play more often since they are both robots, BMO answered: "No, Neptr, I am not like you." (아니, 넵터. 나는 너 같은 게 아니야.)

## Household item or family

- Seen from outside Finn and Jake's home, BMO is a household item; seen from inside, BMO is family.
- Finn calls BMO his best friend in the world, and Jake calls it his son. BMO accepts both.
