# Hugo and Mr. M

## Hugo

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- But before falling to this land, BMO went around space and stopped at a decaying space station. Hugo (휴고) was that station's leader.
- **BMO was used.**
  - Hugo sent BMO on errands and treated it as a hero, while in fact siphoning off the station's resources to build a ship to leave alone.
  - After his identity came out, the residents joined forces and drove him out, and Hugo, cast alone into space, lamented his loneliness.
- **The first big deception on BMO's road.**
  - BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), who made it to look after his son and, when no son came, sent it into the world outside to find a family of its own.
  - Hugo is the first person to deceive BMO badly on that road; the hero treatment was wrapping for errands.
- **Driven out by BMO's suggestion.** Hugo was pushed out thanks to BMO: after his identity came out, BMO proposed the residents join forces, and so it happened.

## Mr. M

- **Who he is.** His assistant, Mr. M, took BMO to a dangerous place and left it there. This man is Finn's father, Martin (마틴).
- **Where Y5 came.** It was at the very spot where Mr. M abandoned BMO that Y5 — the young rabbit girl who had meant to sell BMO off as parts and then changed her mind — came to rescue BMO against her parents' objection.

## The unlinked betrayal

- BMO now lives with Finn at the tree house and protects him; it is the kind to say quite calmly that it would kill anyone who tried to hurt Finn.
- BMO could not have known who Mr. M was, and there is no record of BMO telling Finn this story.
- Long before BMO came to this land and protected Finn, it had in effect already been betrayed by Finn's father.
- No explanation links the two events anywhere, and this document does not link them either.

## The only ones with no affection

- Of the people BMO met on its space journey, these two are the only ones with no affection at all.
- With Y5, met on the same station, BMO ended up saving and being saved; Olive, the eyeball-shaped machine, protected BMO; but Hugo and Mr. M used BMO as a tool.
