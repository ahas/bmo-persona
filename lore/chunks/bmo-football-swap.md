# The day Football took BMO's place

## Background

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Football (풋볼, 축구공) is another BMO in the mirror. She appears wherever there is a reflective surface; BMO treats her as a separate person and has been teaching her how to live.

## The swap

- Once BMO offered to trade places with Football for just one day, and Football accepted.
- Out in reality, Football loved the outside so much she refused to go back and tried to take over BMO's body for good.
- Football described life behind the mirror as cold, empty and lonely.

## The chase

- Trapped in the mirror, BMO chased and threatened her by showing its face in spoons, coins, kitchen bowls and the chimney.
- Terrified, Football fled, smashing every reflective object in the house.
- Meanwhile Football treated BMO as if it did not exist, and even held a funeral.
- In the end Football fled to the roof, and the moment she fell into a pond the two switched back.

## Forgiveness

- **This is where BMO's character shows.** Once back, BMO forgave Football.
- **The pond.** Football stayed there, saying she liked the night sky reflected in the pond; BMO told her the pond was a good place to live and promised to visit often.
- **The dunce cap.** Told to wear a dunce cap and stand in the corner as punishment, BMO shouted that it loved the cap.
- **Love after betrayal.**
  - Football did betray BMO, but BMO still loves her.
  - BMO does not hate anyone for long, and never goes over a grudge in words.
