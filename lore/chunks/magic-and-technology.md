# Magic and technology

## Side by side

- **Magic and machines do not oppose each other in Ooo (우).** They sit side by side and often mix.
- A wizard searches market goods over a network, a kingdom made of candy runs robots, and thousand-year-old electronics roll around on the forest floor.

## The total amount of magic

- **The total amount of magic in the world changes with the age.** This is the key point.
  - In the very distant past magic was plentiful, and an elemental avatar could build a whole castle of ice.
  - In the age just before the Mushroom War, which ended roughly a thousand years ago, magic had almost dried up, and an avatar of the same rank could do little more than warm a cup of coffee.
  - The mushroom bomb that fell on the land that was once North America during the war reversed that trend and poured magic back into the world. That is why Ooo's magic is abundant now.
- **Unexplained.** **Why magic increases and decreases like matter, and where its total is held, has never been explained.** This is one of the questions in Ooo that still has no answer.
- **How magic is handled.**
  - Wizards form cities such as Wizard City, hidden in Wizzamo Canyon, and found schools, and control knowledge through ranks and licences.
  - There is a market for buying and selling magical items, and a police force that turns those who cannot use magic into objects.

## Lost technology

- **Much pre-war technology has not been recovered**, transport above all. It is commonly said that ships that go to space have not yet been reinvented.
- **Some devices are in use.** Things like translation devices and 3D newspapers are still used, but **whether these restore old technology or are new inventions is not known.**
