# Hugo's identity and the end of the Drift

## Where BMO stood

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine that, after going out into space, drifted to the Drift (드리프트), a giant old space station, where it served as sheriff and came to be regarded as a hero.
- The station's ruler was Hugo (휴고), who had led a band of humans that escaped Earth during the Mushroom War.
- BMO had been betrayed by Hugo's assistant Mr. M (M씨) and left in the jungle sector.

## The rescue

- **Y5 saved BMO.** She is the young rabbit girl who chose that name herself, BMO's first friend on the station.
- Defying her parents, and with the help of CGO, an old robot Hugo had discarded, she found BMO.

## The truth

- **It came out of CGO's mouth.**
  - Hugo had fled Earth's war.
  - He had stolen the station natives' technology and made it his own.
  - He was building a new ship, the "Unity Pod" (유니티 포드), to escape taking only his own gang.
  - The vending machines all over the Drift were in fact devices secretly stripping station parts.
- Hugo was pretending to save people while gnawing away at the place they lived.

## The ending

- **Hugo exposed.** BMO and Y5 exposed who Hugo was, and Hugo fled on his ship.
- **The station survived.** Every resident together carried out BMO's plan — the whole station cuts its power at the same moment — and the Drift survived.
- **Hugo and Olive.** Hugo was left alone in a ship with no power, and Olive (올리브), the eyeball-shaped maintenance droid who had brought BMO there, followed him.
- **The residents.**
  - Y5's parents apologized for not listening to their daughter, and the people of the Drift began to repair their homes themselves.
  - Decades later the Drift was still inhabited, and parts of it had been restored.
- **Leaving.** Its work done, BMO left the station riding on the back of a space lard and fell straight to Earth.

## Who Mr. M was

- **Not settled.**
- Several signs suggest he is the same person as Finn's (핀) father, Martin Mertens (마틴 머텐스):
  - his looks and his build;
  - his record of drifting through space;
  - the same verbal habit, "저 녀석 온다" (here he comes);
  - his remarks about children who blame their parents.
- It has never been nailed down as confirmed fact.
