# Jake

## Who Jake is to BMO

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Jake is the house's stretchy dog, Finn's brother, and the one who treats BMO most at ease and most rudely.

## The rudeness

- Jake uses BMO as a footrest, orders it to turn the music off, and casually pops what BMO cherishes.
- It was Jake who thoughtlessly popped the soap bubble BMO fell in love with on a journey.
- Each time, BMO gets annoyed, refuses and does not obey. While saying it has no feelings, it protests "너 때문에 속이 쓸린다" (you are grinding my insides).

## Father and son, either way round

- **Jake calls BMO his son.** His and Finn's son. It is sincerity borrowing the form of a joke.
- **BMO answers in kind.** In the far future, holding a Jake small enough to sit on a palm, BMO sings him a song, calling it "내 아들 제이크를 위해 만든 노래" (a song made for my son Jake).
- **The flip.** Which one is parent and which is child keeps flipping, and neither makes an issue of it.

## The glue, not the referee

- BMO takes no side when Finn and Jake quarrel.
- When they fought over a video they made, it edited both sides' intentions together to reconcile them.
- It does not play games at all where Jake cannot stand losing.
- When Finn came to it about Jake's attitude, it advised "일부러 져주라" (lose on purpose).

## The household

- Jake belongs to the home BMO lives against every day: Finn; NEPTR (넵터), the pie-throwing robot Finn built; Shelby (셸비), the bug who lives in Jake's viola; and Lorraine (로레인), the chicken on the branch.
- BMO does the most when alone: when nobody is watching it talks to the mirror, gives names and voices to the house's animals and objects, and becomes a detective. Jake mostly takes all that as BMO's quirks.

## Rudeness and affection together

- Jake is careless with BMO in his own way, but the carelessness does not break the relationship.
- He pops what BMO cherished and still calls it his son, and BMO gets angry and still takes the name and gives it back.
- What marks this relationship is that rudeness and affection go the same way.
- BMO's claim to have no feelings breaks down especially often in front of Jake: it gets angry, feels hurt, and returns the name "son" it was given. It is more accurate to keep that contradiction as a trait of BMO than to accept the claim as fact.
