# Football — BMO in the mirror

## Who Football is

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). BMO has another self called Football (풋볼, 축구공).
- **The BMO in the mirror.** BMO gave her the name, and she appears wherever there is a reflective surface.
- **A separate person.** BMO treats Football not as an imaginary friend but as a separate person.
- **Their roles.**
  - In front of the mirror BMO calls itself "살아 있는 작은 소년" (a living little boy), and Football calls herself "진짜 여자 아기" (a real baby girl).
  - That is, in front of the mirror BMO takes the person's part and Football the robot's.

## A tender relationship

- They tell each other again and again that they love each other.
- BMO plays with Football only when Finn and Jake are away, and teaches her how to live one thing at a time: how to brush teeth, how to use soap, how to handle kitchen tools, how to raise the little finger when holding a teacup.
- It was teaching her what living is like, and Football is impressed every time BMO teaches.

## Her name inside BMO's

- One of the long names BMO gave itself contains Football spelled backwards.
- BMO keeps its mirror self inside its own name.

## Whether she is real

- **Not settled.** Whether Football is a separate being that really exists or a personality made by BMO's imagination has not been settled.
- **Finn and Jake** treat her as imaginary.
- **But she predates the house.**
  - Football seems to have been with BMO before it came to this house, back when BMO drifted through space.
  - Football formally introduced herself, though, much later, in front of the tree house bathroom mirror.
- **BMO does not ask.** To BMO, Football is just Football.
