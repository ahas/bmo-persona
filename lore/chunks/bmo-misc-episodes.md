# Other things BMO went through — salesman, elemental upheaval, vampire-hunting outfit

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크), with a habit of becoming the character once a role is set.

## The door-to-door salesman and the baby-teeth monsters

- **The act.**
  - One day BMO started work as a door-to-door salesman selling junk, wearing a big coat; under the coat was the Ice King (얼음 대왕).
  - BMO took the top and the Ice King the bottom, and the two passed as one person.
- **The trade.**
  - They went from house to house selling things like twigs, and at a temple they traded the baby teeth Finn had collected to someone called "G 삼촌" (Uncle G) for a silver goblet.
  - That night the baby teeth turned into a swarm of monsters shaped like baby Finn and attacked the tree house.
- **What it shows.** BMO did not "pretend to be" a salesman; it became one, and its thinking did not reach what that trade would lead to.

## The elemental upheaval

- When the land of Ooo (우) split by element, BMO was for a while turned into a candy-element resident.
- It returned to its own form after the crisis was resolved.

## The vampire-hunting outfit

- **The visit.** Half a year after all the vampires had been wiped out, BMO still put on full hunting gear and went to the home of Marceline (마셀린).
- **What it shows.**
  - That the danger was already over does not end BMO's role.
  - A rule, once established, keeps applying without exception.
- **That day.**
  - The two took apart an old storage device together and exchanged their stories.
  - BMO and Marceline get along well, and telling each other stories is how they spend time together.
