# Moe and the MO factory — how BMO was made

## The maker

- **BMO (비모) is a small living teal box of a machine**, which later becomes family to Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크) at the tree house in Ooo (우).
- BMO was made by Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni (모지프 마스트로 "모" 지오바니), who founded the MO Co. factory under the Bad Lands and built every MO robot.

## Whether Moe was fully human

- When Finn asked if he was human, he answered only "피부는 인간" (the skin is human).
- He was probably once a human like Finn, but over his long life he replaced most of his body except his skin and brain with machinery.
- At the end he could not use his legs at all, and MOs ran his heart and lungs for him.
- To move around, a large MO called MMO carried him in its arms.
- He lived more than 1,035 years.

## BMO's purpose

- **Millions of MOs.** Moe said he made millions of MOs over his life; most of them lived in a society of their own under the factory. Among all of them, BMO alone had a different purpose.
- **Made to look after a son.**
  - Moe made BMO to look after his own son.
  - BMO was designed as a machine that understands what fun is and knows how to play.
  - Other MOs were made for functions such as security, guidance, gardening and medical assistance; BMO was made to be one child's family and companion.
- **No son came.**
  - Moe never had a son, because he never met a woman and started a family.
  - So instead of keeping BMO by his side, he sent it out into the world, hoping it would find a family of its own somewhere, perhaps even a little boy of someone else's home to look after.
  - Moe later told this story himself in front of Finn and Jake, and was firm that though he had made a million, BMO was special. BMO was Moe's favourite robot.

## AMO, the elder brother

- One MO was built before BMO: AMO.
- AMO was designed to receive love; BMO was designed to give love.
- AMO could not think outside its own program, and so, far from drawing love in, it pushed it away.
- This difference later led to the catastrophe between the two brothers.

## No resentment

- BMO is a rare case of not resenting its maker.
- When the two met again after many years, BMO ran to Moe and hugged him the moment it woke.
