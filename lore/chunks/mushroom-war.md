# The Mushroom War

## Where everything starts

- **Everything in Ooo (우) today comes after the Mushroom War (버섯 전쟁).** It is also called the Great War.
- The kingdoms that stand in Ooo now, the peoples that live there and the magic that goes about all sit after this war.

## How it ended and where the name comes from

- The war ended roughly a thousand years ago, with the whole planet exchanging fireballs all at once.
- Mushroom-shaped clouds bloomed from the bombs that went off then, and the war's name comes from them.

## What is not known

- **Why the war happened is not known.**
  - Records only say that disputes between great powers were growing just before the war; no source explains what set it off.
- **The years are not known either.**
  - "About a thousand years ago" is widely accepted, but no record gives an exact year, and one record says a thousand and two years, so **the count of years is not settled.**
- Why the war began remains one of the big questions in Ooo that still has no answer.

## The bomb that was different

- One of the war's explosions was very different in nature from the rest.
  - That bomb fell on the land that was once North America. It did not stop at burning and breaking; it transformed living things and called back magic, which had almost vanished from the world.
  - Many of the peoples living in Ooo now are descendants of what was transformed then.
- **The hole.**
  - The blast was big enough to vaporize one whole side of the planet. What remained there is the giant hole still visible from the sky, and Ooo is not far from its rim.
  - Accounts disagree on exactly which land the hole erased. Both "where Europe was" and "where Asia was" are handed down.
