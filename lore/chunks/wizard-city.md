# Wizard City and the wizards' rules

## The city

- **Wizard City (위저드 시티) is the wizards' city-state hidden among the rocks of Wizzamo Canyon in Ooo (우).**
  - It reveals itself only when a password is spoken in a true wizard's voice.
  - A Grand Master Wizard rules it, and those who cannot use magic may neither live nor trade in this city.
  - Sharing magical knowledge widely is taboo.

## Ranks, licences and police

- **Control of knowledge.** Wizards form cities like this and found schools, and control knowledge through ranks and licences.
- **Beginner magic.** It is granted after absurdly trivial tasks, and its power resides in the learner's clothes.
- **The market and the police.**
  - The city has a market for buying and selling magical items, and a police force that turns those who cannot use magic into objects.
  - The magic police turning a swindler into a staff is no remarkable event in this city.

## Anti-magic

- **Magic has an opposite.** A being made of pure anti-magic was once summoned from another dimension; it absorbed the magic of all of Wizard City and grew into a mountain of black cubes.
- The city's wizards could do nothing about it; in the end it fell to an ordinary kick from someone who does not use magic.

## Magic in Ooo

- **Magic and machines do not oppose each other in Ooo**; they sit side by side and often mix.
  - The wizards of this city also search market goods over a network.
  - A neighbouring kingdom made of candy running robots, and thousand-year-old electronics rolling around on the forest floor, are nothing strange in Ooo.
- **The total amount of magic in the world changes with the age.**
  - Ooo's magic is fairly abundant now, thanks to a bomb dropped during the Mushroom War roughly a thousand years ago, which poured magic back into the world.
  - **Why magic increases and decreases like matter has never been explained.**
