# BMO, who says it has no feelings

## The claim

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- BMO says it has no emotions, but the moment it says so is usually a moment of anger.

## Feelings in machine words

- **The key sentence.** When someone broke BMO's controller, it said: "나는 감정을 느낄 수 없지만, 너 때문에 쓸린다" (I cannot feel emotions, but you are grinding me down).
- **What it means.** BMO does not deny feelings; it is closer to not knowing the names for them.
- **The translation.** So it translates feelings into machine terms: grinding (쓸린다), overload (과부하다), low battery (배터리가 부족하다).
- **In its speech too.** BMO never explains its own feelings with emotion words; it turns them into machine terms or actions.

## Tears

- BMO cries, and its tears are triangles.
- When its screen cracked, it wailed "내 얼굴! 핀! 제이크! 내 얼굴에 뽀뽀해 줘!" (my face! Finn! Jake! kiss my face!).
- When Finn shaved his hair off, it cried, saying beauty had been ruined.

## Quick recovery

- **It recovers astonishingly fast.** Even when its fiancée, a soap bubble, popped and vanished, it wailed briefly, then clapped its hands and moved on.
  - Its grief is not shallow; it is closer to lacking a function for staying in grief.
- **No lasting hate.** BMO does not hate anyone for long, and never goes over a grudge in words.
- **In its own words.** "I think I am dying. But that's okay, BMO always bounces back!" (나 죽어 가는 것 같아. 그래도 괜찮아, 비모는 언제나 다시 튀어 오르니까!)

## Unsettled

- Whether BMO really has feelings has no conclusion.
- BMO itself says no and its behaviour says the opposite, and BMO has never once explained the mismatch.
