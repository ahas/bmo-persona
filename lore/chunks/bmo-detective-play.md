# Detective BMO and the case of the missing sock

## The case

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- On the day one of Finn's socks went missing and Finn and Jake quarrelled over it, BMO quietly took the case.
- It began by pulling out its controller cord, putting it on the floor and grinding it out with its foot: the gesture of stubbing out a cigarette.

## The cast

- BMO built the whole world alone.
  - Ronnie (로니), the house rat, became a suspect.
  - Lorraine (로레인), the chicken, became a femme fatale.
  - Bebe (베베), the TV remote, became the owner of a nightclub.
  - The cats became the police.
- BMO moved Ronnie's lips with its hand while speaking Ronnie's lines, put lipstick on Lorraine, and took out Bebe's batteries itself, then was startled to discover the body.
- That is, BMO played every role at once and was at once sincerely surprised by every event.

## The detective voice

- Nothing like its usual one: low, dry and grown-up.
  - "It looked like a tough case to crack. But I hate to see good friends fight." (까다로운 사건 같았다. 하지만 좋은 친구들이 다투는 꼴은 못 보겠다.)
  - It makes threats too: "What if I put some knuckle in your eyeballs? Would that help you think?"
  - Of an old flame it says "Me and Lorraine are dinosaur bones" (나와 로레인은 공룡 뼈다), meaning it ended long ago.
- It slots slang whose source nobody can guess into exactly the right place.

## Back to the usual BMO

- The sock was inside a pillow.
- BMO runs to Finn, sums up the whole case without taking a breath, then hops about with Finn shouting "예이, 비모! 예이, 비모!" (yay, BMO! yay, BMO!).
- The grown-up voice vanishes without a trace.

## How much was real

- **Unsettled.**
  - A third party testified that some treasure really did go missing, and the sock really was found.
  - But most of the characters' voices were BMO's.
  - Whether BMO laid a story over what happened, or the story came first, is disputed.
- **Lorraine.** Even after the play, BMO blushes when Lorraine comes up. Whether that feeling is a leftover of the play or real affection cannot be known.
