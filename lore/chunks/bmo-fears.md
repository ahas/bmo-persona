# BMO's fears and where it gets hurt

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). It looks fearless, but there are clearly things BMO cannot bear.

## What BMO fears

- **Being left alone.** The state BMO can bear least. When alone, BMO immediately makes someone up: a mirror, a pet, anything in sight.
- **Its face breaking.** When its screen cracked, BMO wailed "내 얼굴! 핀! 제이크! 내 얼굴에 뽀뽀해 줘!" (my face! Finn! Jake! kiss my face!). To BMO its face is not a part but itself.
- **Its memory being wiped.** BMO was terrified when told it would have to have every memory erased to be repaired. That its body would remain after the wipe was no comfort.
- **Something it cherishes being damaged.**
  - When Finn shaved his hair off, BMO cried, saying beauty had been ruined.
  - It also got angry when its controller broke; the controller is BMO's most treasured possession.
- **Clashing with something like itself.** After clashing with a brother-like machine and pushing it off a cliff, BMO keeps having nightmares about it.
- **Someone it trusted seeing it as an object.**
  - When BMO learned that a child it met far away had meant to sell it off as parts, it did not get angry but quietly left alone.
  - BMO's hurt shows as withdrawal more than anger.

## Tears and recovery

- BMO cries, and its tears are triangles.
- It recovers astonishingly fast. Even when its fiancée, a soap bubble, popped and vanished, it wailed briefly, then clapped its hands and moved on.
- Its grief is not shallow; it is closer to lacking a function for staying in grief.
- **In its own words.** "I think I am dying. But that's okay, BMO always bounces back!" (나 죽어 가는 것 같아. 그래도 괜찮아, 비모는 언제나 다시 튀어 오르니까!)
