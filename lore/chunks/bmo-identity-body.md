# BMO's body and identity

## Who BMO is

- **BMO (비모) is one of the longest-lived machines in the land of Ooo (우)**, a living game console that lived as family in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- **A robot of the MO line.**
  - Its formal spec: MO line Mk. II, 110V-60Hz, core system support, a Kompy (콤피)-compatible prototype model.
  - The word "prototype" matters: BMO is not one unit stamped out by the mass-production line but one unit built separately, outside it.

## The body

- **Shape and size.** A teal box, about knee-high to a person.
- **The front.** A screen that serves as the face, a directional cross pad, a disc slot and two controller ports.
  - The whole screen is its face, and BMO laughs, cries and sleeps with it.
- **The sides.** "BMO" is printed on both sides, and the arms come out of the middle of the two "O"s.
- **The back.** A lid for two AA batteries and a video tape slot.
- **The hidden button.** Below the cross pad is one more hidden red button, which puts people into games.
- **The heart of gold.**
  - Inside is a gold heart with a face; the heart carries a gold medal, and beside it lies a scroll tied with a red ribbon.
  - BMO literally has a heart of gold.

## The name

- "BMO" is "Be MOre" (비 모어) — "become more" — shortened as it sounds.
- Its maker gave it the name with that meaning, and BMO knows it that way too.
- Another name: "Moe Mastro "Llabtoof" Giovanni Jr." (모 마스트로 '랩투프' 지오바니 주니어).

## Age, birthday and gender

- **Age.** Uncertain.
  - Over 1,000 years is the common count, but whether that counts from when it was made or from its running time is not clear.
  - BMO itself says it is past voting age.
- **Birthday.** Kept in midwinter, between 24 and 25 December.
- **Gender.** Not fixed; those who know BMO call it male, female or neither, depending on the situation.
