# The widely known regions of Ooo

## Overview

- **Ooo (우) is a land of kingdoms large and small side by side**, and between and beyond them lie wide zones that belong to no kingdom.
- The regions people have actually visited, and about which stories remain, are listed below.

## The regions

- **The Grass Lands (풀의 땅)** — the green plains at the heart of Ooo.
  - They border the Candy Kingdom, the Ice Kingdom and the Fire Kingdom, so they are in effect Ooo's crossroads.
  - Many homes, the tree fort among them, are here, and small nations such as the Goblin Kingdom and the Hot Dog Kingdom are scattered here too.
  - Old maps sometimes label them "the green plains" (푸른 평원).
- **The Bad Lands (배드랜드)** — the dry wasteland to the south; the Desert of Doom takes up most of it.
  - Despite appearances it is not wholly empty: small settlements hide there, such as the Slime Kingdom, the Breakfast Kingdom and the Glass Kingdom.
  - The rest belongs to drifters and bandits.
- **The Desert Lands (사막의 땅)** — the dry region northeast of the Bad Lands. The Breakfast Kingdom, with its castle shaped like a coffee filter, is here.
- **The Cotton Candy Forest (면사탕 숲) and the Rock Candy Mountains** — the area surrounding the Candy Kingdom, whose inside is all candy and sweets, outside its walls.
  - The forest's trees are edible whole, which often startles outsiders.
- **The Evil Forest, the Spooky Forest and the Ghost Swamp** — zones that belong to no kingdom and are dangerous in themselves.
- **Wizzamo Canyon (위즈와모 협곡)** — the rock canyon where the city of wizards hides.

## Beyond these regions

- Several seas and islands lie beyond them whose names appear only on two kinds of map, and which nobody claims to have visited.
- Almost nothing is known about such places beyond their names.
