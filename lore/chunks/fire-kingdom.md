# The Fire Kingdom

## The land and its people

- **The Fire Kingdom (불의 왕국) is a kingdom in the southwest of Ooo (우) made only of volcanoes and seas of lava.** The royal family lives in the Fire Palace.
- **Its people.** Most residents consider themselves evil and are not ashamed of it.
- **The border with the Ice Kingdom.** The Ice Kingdom of sharp snowy peaks lies to the north of Ooo, and the Fire Kingdom borders it, so between the two lands lies a strip with neither snow nor fire.

## The throne passes by force

- Ooo's kingdoms are usually ruled by one king or princess with hereditary thrones, but the Fire Kingdom is different: here the throne passes by force.
- The present Flame Princess (화염 공주) drove out her own father to take the throne, and he in turn had become king by snuffing out his brother.
- **The fire giants.** Sleeping fire giants lie beneath the kingdom and were regarded as the last-resort weapon, but most have been destroyed.

## Ooo around it

- No government covers all of Ooo, and there is no continent-wide army.
- Each kingdom arranges its own army and police, and they meet regularly at "the Royal Council of Ooo" (우 왕족 대회의). **What is discussed there is not known outside.**

## Fire as an element

- **Fire is counted as one of Ooo's four elements.** Since life began on Earth, each of the four elements — fire, ice, candy and slime — has had an avatar that takes the element as its body, and the fire seat, too, is mostly filled in every age.
- **The elemental upheaval.** Once elemental power was used to the full: all of Ooo split into four zones and every resident turned into a being of one element. Those who became fire then attacked anyone.

## Places known only by name

- Maps show the Steam Island off the Fire Kingdom's coast and Chicken Blood Bay south of it, but **almost nothing is known about them beyond their names.**
