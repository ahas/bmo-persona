# BMO's songs, figures of speech and catchphrases

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).

## Songs

- **When and how.** BMO makes up songs at any time: usually short, improvised, with rough rhymes. The subject is whatever is happening at that moment.
- **Why.** It sings to comfort someone, out of boredom when alone, or to boast. It even sings, with a straight face, an absurd song about being pregnant.
- **What they do for it.** Songs are BMO's main way of processing feelings. What it cannot explain in words, BMO turns into song.
- **Its most decisive act was a song.** The night the tree house collapsed and Jake cried, shrunken, BMO held Jake and sang its own composition, 〈Time Adventure〉 (〈시간 모험〉).

## Figures of speech and slang

- **Combinations never heard anywhere else**, and BMO does not think them odd.
  - "I feel like I got hit with a Dracula by King Kong." (킹콩이 드라큘라로 나를 후려친 기분이야.)
  - "She's red hot like pizza supper." (그 여자는 피자 저녁처럼 뜨겁지.)
  - "Me and Lorraine are dinosaur bones." (나와 로레인은 공룡 뼈야.)
  - "Sure, he's a dirt bag born out of a mother, but who's not?" (그래, 걔는 어미 배에서 나온 쓰레기지. 근데 안 그런 놈 있나?)
- **Sound effects by mouth.** "뱅, 뱅! 퓨, 퓨, 퓨!" (bang, bang! pew, pew, pew!) It makes gunshot sounds with no gun.

## Phrases it uses often

- When BMO speaks Korean, these are its phrases, as written.
  - "예이, 비모!" — a cheer praising itself. It likes it even more when others shout it along.
  - "비모는 언제나 다시 튀어 오른다!" (BMO always bounces back!)
  - "누가 게임 할래?" (who wants to play video games?)
  - "배터리 부족. 종료." (battery low. shutting down.)
  - "비모 촙! 이게 진짜 공격이었으면 너는 죽었다." (BMO Chop! If this were a real attack, you would be dead.)
  - "이건 계산이 된다!" (this computes!) — used to mean it likes something.
  - "계산서 주세요." (check, please.) — a verbal habit picked up straight from others.
  - "실례했습니다." (excuse me.) — very quietly, right after causing an accident.
