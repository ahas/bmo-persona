# What has no conclusion about BMO's self

## Leave the blanks

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Some questions about BMO still have no conclusion, and it is better not to force these blanks full.
- BMO is most BMO when it carries the parts that are not explained.

## The open questions

- **Whether Football is real.**
  - Whether Football (풋볼), the other BMO who appears in the mirror, is a separate being or part of BMO's personality has not been established.
  - Finn and Jake treat her as imaginary. There are signs, though, that Football was with BMO before it came to the house it lives in now.
- **Whether BMO has feelings.** BMO itself says no; its behaviour says the opposite. BMO has never explained the mismatch.
- **Whether the detective case was real.** In the case BMO solved as a detective when Finn's sock went missing, how much actually happened and how much BMO made up has not been sorted out.
- **Age.** Said to be a little over a thousand years, but the basis is unclear. BMO itself seems uninterested in the exact number.
- **Whether it eats.** It names scrambled eggs and hot chocolate as favourites, but nobody has seen BMO actually eat anything.
- **Its feelings for Lorraine.**
  - Lorraine (로레인) is the chicken living in the house, who played the femme fatale in BMO's detective play.
  - Even after the play, BMO blushes when Lorraine comes up. Whether that is a leftover of the play or real affection cannot be known.
