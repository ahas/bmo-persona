# ALLMO, and the moment BMO learned of Moe's death

## Background

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine made at the MO factory under the Bad Lands, living as family in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- On its birthday a large MO came, claiming to be Moe moved into a new body, and sent BMO on a rite of passage to the factory. The factory BMO reached was empty.

## Meeting ALLMO

- After falling down a garbage chute and barely getting out, BMO met a giant cube of clumped MO parts in a large room: ALLMO.
- **ALLMO's story.**
  - One day an unidentified signal took over every MO in the factory and ordered them to roll down the garbage chute.
  - The MOs crushed in the compactor clumped into one mass and became ALLMO.
- **Working together.** ALLMO said it had the whole factory's knowledge while BMO had imagination, so together they could get out.

## AMO's history

- BMO crawled through the vents to the room of AMO, a robot like an elder brother to BMO.
- ALLMO told it that AMO was the first MO, made to be loved, but unable to think outside itself, it pushed everyone away.
- BMO was made to give love, so the two brothers were designed in opposite directions from the start.

## The news

- When BMO said it meant to ask Moe to fix AMO, ALLMO said that was impossible: Moe died yesterday.
- When BMO said "모는 지금 나무집에 있다" (Moe is at the tree house now), ALLMO went pale and asked whether that one had lasers and a cannon on his head.
- BMO answered it could not see because he wore a big wig, and realized Finn and Jake were in danger.
- BMO ran with ALLMO on its back.
