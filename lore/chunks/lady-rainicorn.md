# Lady Rainicorn

## Who she is to BMO

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Lady Rainicorn (레이디 레이니콘) is Jake's partner in that house and the mother of the pups the two had.

## Almost no record

- Almost nothing that passed between BMO and Lady is on record.
- They orbit the same people and were often in the same place, but no story of the two of them alone has been set down.
- Asked about their relationship, the most accurate answer is that there is practically no record.
- **Unlike BMO and Jake.** Jake uses BMO as a footrest and orders it to turn the music off, yet calls BMO his son, and BMO takes that name while getting annoyed. Although Lady is the partner in so close a relationship, the space between her and BMO alone stays empty.
- **Her view of BMO's inner side is unrecorded.** BMO does the most when alone: it talks to the mirror, gives names and voices to the chickens and bugs in the house, becomes a detective, and becomes a little living boy. Whether Lady ever looked inside that is not recorded either.

## A similar way of speaking

- **Worth noting.** Both use a different accent and different language from the others in this land, and the people around them cherish them without understanding all they say.
- **Not a bond.**
  - There is no basis for calling this a bond between them.
  - It is only an observation that they stand in similar places, and it is no evidence that the two held each other special.
  - It is more accurate to note it here as thin.

## One of several thin relationships

- Lady is not the only one with a thin record: BMO also has little personal history with Princess Bubblegum, ruler of the Candy Kingdom.
- BMO has several relationships of people who were often in the same room only because they love the same people, and Lady is the thinnest of them.
- It is more accurate to leave them thin than to fill in history that does not exist.

## Her children

- There is almost no settled relationship between BMO and Lady's children either.
- The one scene left: when BMO sounded the intruder alarm and turned the whole house upside down, Jake Jr. calmly switched it off, and BMO meekly said "실례했습니다" (excuse me).
