# The Land of the Dead

## The best-known realm

- In Ooo (우), death is not an end but a move, and a dead soul goes to one of many layers called the Dead Worlds. The best known of those realms is **the Land of the Dead (죽은 자들의 땅)**.
- **The living can go too.** A door opens when one stares cross-eyed at a corner where two walls meet.
- **Where it is.** **Whether it lies in caves under Ooo or in an entirely different dimension has not been established.**

## What is there

- A long escalator leads down, and a gatekeeper guards its entrance.
- Bones, wrecked patrol cars and crashed flying saucers are scattered over the land.
- Skeleton people hungry for flesh live there, with all kinds of bugs and monsters.
- **The River of Forgetting (망각의 강)** runs across the land. Whoever drinks its water loses every memory.

## Death's castle

- **The castle.** The river flows into Death's castle, which is built of light; inside are a garden set with stones and a stage set with instruments.
- **Death.** Death, who lives in the castle, is the cosmic being who rules every realm of the afterlife.
  - He has a head like a horse's skull and dresses in a western style.
  - Within his realm he is practically omnipotent: he can revive the dead and uses a kiss that returns memories.
- **The music duel.**
  - To those who seek him out to gain something, Death offers a music duel: lose and you are executed, win and you get what you want. That is why instruments stand on the castle's stage.
  - Going down to this land of skeletons and winning back the dead with a music duel really happens in Ooo.

## Open question

- The layer known as where Death stays is Dead World 37, but whether it is the same place as the Land of the Dead **is not clear.**
