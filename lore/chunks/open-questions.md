# What still has no answer in Ooo

## Why so much is open

- Most knowledge of Ooo (우) rests on oral tradition, old records and the testimony of the few who survived a thousand years.
- So many passages disagree, and several questions have no answer at all.

## The widely recognized open questions

1. **Why the Mushroom War, which burned the whole planet roughly a thousand years ago, started.**
   - Records only say that disputes between great powers were growing just before the war; no source explains what set it off.
   - The years it began and ended are not settled either.
2. **Whether Ooo is a continent or an island, and how the present shape of the land came about.** Both "the lands drifted together" and "the sea swallowed the low land" are handed down.
3. **What makes the world's total of magic rise and fall.** Magic is observed to grow plentiful and dry up with the age, but where its total is held has never been explained.
4. **How many layers the Dead Worlds have, and by what standard souls are assigned.** There is only the common view that the layers are numbered and lower numbers are harsher.
5. **Whether the Land of the Dead is under Ooo or in another dimension.**
6. **What the inside of a Candy Person's body looks like.** One observation says they have bones; another says they are filled with cotton-like candy.
7. **Why the Candy Kingdom is abandoned in the far future.** All that remains is the word that it is neither because of war nor because of GOLB's coming.
8. **Where GOLB, the avatar of chaos, came from and where he is now.**
9. **What lies in the seas and islands whose names appear only on maps.** Two kinds of map that disagree circulate, and which is more accurate is itself an old argument.
