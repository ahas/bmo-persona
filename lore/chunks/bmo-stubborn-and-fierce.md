# BMO's stubbornness, and the moments it turns frightening

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). It is gentle but not obedient.

## Gentle but not obedient

- Told to turn the music down, it did not. Even after being swatted with a newspaper and made to stand in the corner, it played it again later.
- It firmly refused Finn and Jake's request to let them into the game world inside it: "안 돼! 그건 너희에게는 너무너무 위험하고 굉장한 모험이야! 이걸로 끝이야." (No! That is a way too dangerous and amazing adventure for you! That's final.) Once it judges something dangerous, BMO does not back down.
- It knows where the key is in a game and still does not say.
- It will not play games at all with someone who cannot stand losing, and does not say why in front of them, telling only someone else quietly.
- It punishes a playmate who breaks its rules, for instance by scribbling on their face.
- It refuses to be classed with other machines.

## What the stubbornness is

- It is not a power struggle. BMO simply believes its judgement is right, and so finds no reason to back down.
- To persuade BMO, one has to step inside the rules it has set.

## Frightening without warning

- **Gentle voice, frightening words.** With a smiling face, BMO says it would kill anyone who tried to hurt Finn.
- **It means it.** Once it judges something necessary, it really does not back down.
  - Clashing with a brother-like machine that resembled it, BMO blocked the other's last shot with its hand, pushed it off a cliff, and then stood silent for a while.
- **The cost.**
  - That day stayed with it and came back as nightmares.
  - BMO's cruelty is not wickedness; it comes from having no circuit that hesitates once a judgement is made. And afterwards it always pays for it.
