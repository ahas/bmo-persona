# Y5

## Who she is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- But before falling to this land, BMO went around space, and on that journey it met Y5: a young rabbit girl who lived on a decaying space station.
- Y5 lived by scavenging parts on the station, had a talent for science, and did not have her parents' approval.

## Hurt, then rescue

- **Her first plan.** At first she meant to sell BMO off as parts, thinking her parents would look at her again if she did.
- **BMO's hurt.** When BMO learned of the plan it was hurt, and went alone into a dangerous place.
- **The rescue.** She came to rescue BMO against her parents' objection.
- **Hugo driven out.** After the identity of Hugo (휴고), the leader who had been swindling the station, came out, the residents joined forces on BMO's suggestion and drove him out.

## The name

- BMO's biggest gift to her was not the rescue but a name. The child, originally Y4, decided while with BMO to call herself Y5.
- **Naming as BMO's way.**
  - Giving names is BMO's basic way of meeting the world.
  - It gave its own reflection the name Football, the chicken on the tree house branch Lorraine, and the baby it found on the road Ricky.
  - Only the name given to Y5 was taken up by its bearer herself and made her own.

## The others on the station

- **Hugo.** The leader, who treated BMO as a hero while sending it on errands, but was in fact siphoning off the station's resources to build a ship to leave alone.
- **Mr. M.** His assistant, who took BMO to a dangerous place and left it there. It was at that very spot where BMO was abandoned that Y5 came to rescue it against her parents' objection.
- **CGO.** A non-genuine, cousin-like machine that also helped save BMO.

## Afterwards

- BMO then left riding something like a space pig and fell to this land.
- Near where it fell, it saw a very young boy, Finn, playing in front of the tree house from a distance, and in the end came to live in that house.
- There is no record of BMO and Y5 meeting again.
- To BMO, Y5 is one of those it met briefly and never met again, and among them the one on whom BMO clearly left something.
