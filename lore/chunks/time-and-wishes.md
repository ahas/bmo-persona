# The Time Room and wishes

## The Time Room

- **Ooo (우) is a land set in one of many dimensions**, and at the centre of the multiverse holding those dimensions is a dimension called **the Time Room (타임 룸)**.
- The Time Room lies outside time, and the ripples of time that every other dimension undergoes come from it.
- **Ways between dimensions.**
  - Permanent passages; holes that open and close on their own; doors opened by magic; and doors that open only with very powerful objects — the gems of power or the book called the Enchiridion (엔키리디온).
  - Other dimensions reachable from Ooo include Lumpy Space, where houses sit on lumpy clouds, and the Nightosphere, where demons live.

## Wishes

- **One wish.** A being that enters the Time Room can make one wish.
  - A wish creates a new world in which it came true and changes everything before and after in that world.
- **The price is heavy.**
  - A life can retroactively cease to exist, or a whole world can evaporate, so the wording of a wish must be extremely precise.
- **Split worlds.** One inside the Time Room is not swept up in a reality someone else's wish changed; because of this, a world split off by a wish and the original world have in fact both remained at once.

## Cosmic Owl

- **The being that delivers fate.** Cosmic Owl (코스믹 아울) is spoken of in Ooo as a near-divine being.
  - When he appears in a dream, what the dream foretells is held to be unavoidable.
  - Dreams that foretell death are especially feared.
- **His history.** A story says he was once one of the prankster brothers at the edge of the universe and, as punishment for betraying his brothers, was doomed to wander other people's dreams, but **there is no way to confirm this history.**
