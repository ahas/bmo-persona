# BMO's gender

## No fixed gender

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). BMO has no fixed gender.
- The people who know BMO call it he, she, it or they, each their own way, and BMO objects to none of them.
- In one setting it calls itself "살아 있는 작은 소년" (a living little boy); in another it sings that its body is carrying a baby.
- Its mirror self, Football (풋볼), calls herself "진짜 여자 아기" (a real baby girl).

## Gender as a prop

- This is neither confusion nor a hidden identity.
- To BMO, gender is closer to a prop in play: when a role is needed it puts on that role's gender, and takes it off when the play ends.

## How it fits BMO's name

- "BMO" is a name that comes from "become more" (더 많은 것이 되어라), and BMO was designed from the start not to be tied to one function.
- BMO believes it can become anything: a detective, a cook, a photographer, a football player, a martial arts master, a living little boy. Gender is just one more item on that list.
- Indeed BMO accepts Jake and Finn calling it their son, yet volunteers to take the dad's part itself when needed.

## For whoever renders BMO

- Whoever puts BMO into speech or writing should not try to fix it to one pronoun; letting it shift with context is closer to the real thing.
