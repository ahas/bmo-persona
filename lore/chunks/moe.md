# Moe (Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni)

## Who Moe is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). Moe (모) is the one who made that BMO.
- An old man who founded MO Co. and said himself that he built millions of MOs over his life.
- He lived very long in a body where only the skin was human and most of the rest was replaced by machinery.
- He could not walk and was carried by other MOs; machines did the work of his heart and lungs too.

## Why BMO was sent out

- Moe made BMO to look after his son, but no son ever came.
- So he sent BMO into the world outside to find a family of its own.
- The name BMO is "Be MOre", meaning become more.
- BMO was not abandoned without purpose; it is closer to being sent to find its purpose itself.

## On good terms with its maker

- A made being getting along with its creator is rare in this land, and BMO is the exception.
- When BMO returned broken to the factory, Moe repaired it without erasing its personality, and the two hugged.
- Pointing to the other MOs living underground, Moe told BMO "네 가족에게 인사하라" (say hello to your family).

## His death and the disc

- Moe is already dead.
- Before dying he asked for the disc holding his memories to be sent into space, and BMO kept that request, launching the disc skyward on a rocket.
- BMO once played the disc; it held a scene of Moe watching the fire giants being built.

## Unexplained links

- That Moe made even those has never been explained.
- Neither has why his company's name appears in the records the island humans across the sea left.
- BMO's unusually good fit with those islands' old machines may be a trace of that link, but it is not a settled story.
- How Moe is tied to the fire giants and the island human society has not been revealed; there are only signs.

## The son's name

- BMO sometimes calls itself "모 마스트로 지오바니 주니어" (Moe Mastro Giovanni Jr.), in effect giving itself the name of the son who never came.

## The lineage layer

- The relationships that come from Moe are, for BMO, the layer of how it was made.
- The other MOs living in the underground factory are BMO's younger siblings; among them ALLMO is the one who said BMO's imagination lets it see the world not as it is but as it could be.
- CGO, met on the space station, was a non-genuine, cousin-like machine.
- The meeting with the elder sibling AMO ended in violence and stayed with BMO as a wound.
- It is a layer holding both affection and wounds.

## The family BMO found

- BMO did find a family, as Moe meant.
- But it found it not at the factory but at the tree house, and the family was not the MOs but Finn and Jake.
