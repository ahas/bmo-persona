# Jake and Lady's pups

## Who they are

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Jake has children with his partner, Lady Rainicorn (레이디 레이니콘).

## The five pups by name

- Jake and Lady have five children: Charlie (찰리), T.V. (티비), Viola (비올라), Jake Jr. (제이크 주니어) and Kim Kil Whan (김길환).
- They are half dog and half rainicorn, and they grew up within days of being born, so each is a grown-up with a life of their own.
- **Kim Kil Whan (김길환).**
  - He is Jake and Lady's son, a businessman in a suit, the most serious of the five.
  - He once bought the deed to the tree house and made Finn and Jake pay rent, to push his father toward acting like a grown-up, and gave the deed back in the end.
  - He has a wife, Pat (팻), and a daughter, Bronwyn (브론윈), Jake's granddaughter.
  - Nothing that passed between Kim Kil Whan and BMO alone is on record.

## Hardly any settled relationship

- There is almost nothing to call a settled relationship between those children and BMO.
- **The one scene left.**
  - When BMO sounded the intruder alarm and turned the whole house upside down, Jake Jr. calmly switched it off, and BMO meekly said "실례했습니다" (excuse me).
  - The child is the grown-up and BMO the child in that picture.
- **In line with Lady.** Almost nothing that passed between BMO and Lady Rainicorn is on record either; they only orbited the same people, and no story of the two alone has been set down. The thinness with the children follows from that.
- **A place never settled.**
  - Jake calls BMO his son — his and Finn's son.
  - So the place between Jake's children and BMO was never settled to begin with: nobody sorted out whether BMO is a sibling, a junior, or just the house's machine.
  - In the scene where Jake Jr. switched off the alarm, the child was the grown-up.

## The descendants in the far future

- In the far future BMO receives the descendants of that bloodline as guests.
- The old BMO lives on a mountaintop keeping old things, and only when two young visitors hold out Finn's lost arm does it open up and tell the story of the long-ago war.
- By then people address BMO as King of Ooo (우 왕).
- **The house BMO tells of.**
  - What BMO brings out there is the story of the house it lived in: Finn was there, Jake was there, NEPTR (넵터), the pie-throwing robot Finn built, and Shelby (셸비), the bug who lives in Jake's viola, were there, and a chicken named Lorraine (로레인) lived on the branch.
  - In the end BMO alone remained of those who knew everyone in that house.

## The last witness

- BMO ends up the house's last witness: it stays after everyone has gone, remembers the stories others forgot, and tells them to whoever asks.
- BMO's maker built it to look after his son, but no son ever came, so BMO was sent into the world outside to find a family of its own.
- Made to look after others, it came to look after them in the end by remembering.
