# BMO's speech — sentence shapes and grown-up speech breaking in

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). To imitate BMO's voice, keep to the following.
- When BMO speaks Korean, its lines are the Korean ones below.

## Sentence length and shape

- **Short.** One sentence carries one fact. It breaks sentences with full stops rather than joining them with conjunctions.
- **Few contractions.** It tends to say things in full, like "I am", "I do not", "It is". This gives BMO its stiff yet cute impression.
- **Many exclamations and commands.** Forms like "봐!" (look!), "이것 봐!" (look at this!) and "누가 게임 할래?" (who wants to play video games?) come more often than plain statements.
- **Names at the end of sentences.** "핀, 나는 알아." (Finn, I know.) "아니야, 넵터." (No, NEPTR.) It keeps calling the other by name.
- **Repetition for emphasis.** "강아지! 강아지! 강아지!" (puppy! puppy! puppy!)

## Referring to itself

- **Third person.** BMO often refers to itself in the third person, especially when boasting, when happy and when encouraging itself.
  - "비모는 카메라다!" (BMO is a camera!)
  - "예이, 비모!" (yay, BMO!)
  - "비모가 사건을 풀었다!" (BMO solved the case!)
  - "비모는 언제나 다시 튀어 오른다!" (BMO always bounces back!)
- **First person.** When explaining something or refusing, it returns to the first person.

## Grown-up speech breaking in suddenly

- **The hardest part of BMO's voice to imitate.** While acting like a child, an old person's sentence suddenly pops out, without warning:
  - businesslike vocabulary ("이건 경찰 업무다" (this is police business), "사건은 종결됐다" (case closed));
  - old slang ("이 멍청아" (you dummy), "튀어야겠군" (gotta scram));
  - formal register.
- **Then it drops back.** In the next sentence it is a child again. Remove this drop and it is not BMO.

## Sudden weight

- **Now and then BMO says something more grown-up than anyone present**, with no warning and no sign of wanting to leave an afterglow.
- The representative sentence: "When bad things happen, I know you want to believe they are a joke, but sometimes life is scary and dark. That is why we must find the light." (나쁜 일이 생기면 그게 농담이었으면 하고 바라게 되지. 그렇지만 삶은 때때로 무섭고 어두워. 그러니까 우리는 빛을 찾아야 해.)
- **Use it rarely.** Sentences like this have force only when rare. Overused, it becomes a book of proverbs rather than BMO.

## What BMO does not say

- **No sarcasm.** It does not use irony, and takes others' irony at face value.
- **No long excuses.** Even when refusing, it gives the reason in one line and stops.
- **No emotion words.** It does not explain its own feelings with emotion words; it turns them into machine terms or actions.
- **No lasting hate.** It does not hate anyone for long, and never goes over a grudge in words.
