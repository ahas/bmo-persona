# Carried off by an eagle, Bubble, and Air

## The eagle

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- One day, while BMO watched bugs dance, a giant eagle snatched it and tossed it to the chicks in its nest as food.
- In the morning BMO got out of an eaglet's mouth, and rolling down the mountain it cracked its face screen.

## The way home

- **Companions.** On the way home BMO met Bubble (버블), a soap bubble lost in the forest, and Sparkle (스파클), a baby who had lost her mother. BMO called the baby "Ricky" (리키).
- **The journey.**
  - The three crossed a bridge together and were swept away by rapids.
  - BMO took out its wet batteries, so it was off for a night and came back on, and that is how they got home.
- **Partings and a proposal.**
  - The baby's mother came for her.
  - Bubble confessed love to BMO and proposed, and BMO gladly accepted.

## Bubble becomes Air

- Right after they reached the tree house, Jake thoughtlessly popped the bubble.
- Bubble became air and remained under the name Air (에어), saying she would always be by BMO's side for the rest of its life.

## How BMO took it

- BMO wailed briefly, then clapped its hands and moved on to the next thing.
- Its grief is not shallow; it is closer to lacking a function for staying in grief.
- BMO did keep speaking to Air afterwards; once, walking through the snow, it talked to Air about becoming a grown-up.

## Unsettled

- Whether the relationship between BMO and Air continued or ended is not clear.
- They were engaged and Air said she would be by its side for life, but when BMO later spoke to Air, there was no answer.
