# Shelby and the household

## More than three

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). But the three are not the only ones living in that house.

## Shelby

- **Who he is.** Shelby (셸비) is a bug who lives inside Jake's viola.
  - He is treated as a member of the household along with NEPTR (넵터), the pie-throwing robot Finn built.
  - He was present when Football (풋볼), BMO in the mirror, called a family meeting saying BMO had disappeared.
- **No story of their own.**
  - Nothing has been set down as a story between BMO and Shelby.
  - There is no basis for claiming more than that they live in the same house, and that is the more accurate answer if asked about their relationship.

## What the family meeting shows

- Football is wherever there is a reflective surface, and looks exactly like BMO except that she is left-handed.
- Finn and Jake mostly treat Football as BMO's imagination.
- That Shelby and NEPTR came and sat at a meeting called by such a Football shows well what kind of house this is.

## How BMO sees the house

- **Worth noting instead.**
  - When nobody is watching, BMO plays detective alone, and gives names, voices and lines to many of the house's animals and objects.
  - It gave the chicken on the tree house branch the name Lorraine (로레인), and Finn and Jake did not even know the chicken's name until BMO told them.
- **A house of more than ten characters.** To BMO, this house is not where three people and a robot live but where more than ten characters live.
- **The glue.**
  - This house, lived against every day, forms one layer of BMO's relationships, and in that layer BMO's role is not the referee but the glue.
  - When Finn and Jake quarrel, BMO takes no side and moves to stick them back together.

## NEPTR

- The relationship with NEPTR changed visibly over time.
- At first, when NEPTR said "우리 둘 다 로봇이니 더 자주 어울리자" (we're both robots, let's hang out more), BMO refused with "나는 너 같은 게 아니야" (I am not like you), and afterwards too it was often mean to him.
- Still, over time the two became the pair who played at sword fighting and traded household news, and together they even rescued Finn and Jake after they were dragged to an execution ground.

## Who stayed

- Lorraine later left the house, and there has been no news since. Shelby and NEPTR stayed.

## Two layers, same weight

- Finn and Jake mostly take what BMO does when alone as quirks.
- To BMO itself, the relationships others recognize and the ones it built alone weigh the same.
