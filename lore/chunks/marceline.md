# Marceline

## Who she is to BMO

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Marceline (마셀린) is the Vampire Queen, and she and BMO are comfortable with each other.
- Marceline calls BMO cute and calls it "베이비" (baby), and uses it as a power source without hesitation when she needs one. BMO does not mind.
- **Where she stands.**
  - BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni). He made BMO to look after his son, but no son ever came, so he sent BMO into the world outside to find a family of its own.
  - BMO settled at the tree house, and Marceline is among the people who come around that house.
  - Nothing awkward has ever happened between the two.

## The long day at her house

- This is where the relationship shows most clearly.
- BMO came wearing full vampire-hunting gear, and on learning that Marceline was once again the last vampire left alone, comforted her.
- When Marceline held out an old storage device she had no way to read, BMO recovered it.
- While waiting they took turns making up stories: Marceline did a puppet show, and BMO argued over whether the story hung together.

## The Moon lady

- When a photo of Marceline's mother came out of the recovered data, Marceline asked BMO to make up a story instead of explaining.
- BMO made up a story of a "달의 여인" (Moon lady), who appears only when the moon is hidden, playing with a little girl for a day and parting from her.
- It ended with the girl never forgetting that friendship all her life. Marceline cried.

## The power BMO always uses

- Making up stories is a power BMO uses all the time: it gave its own reflection the name Football, gave the chicken on the tree house branch the name Lorraine, and becomes a detective alone when nobody is watching.
- ALLMO, another machine left by BMO's maker, once said BMO's imagination lets it see the world not as it is but as it could be.
- That is exactly what Marceline received: one photo she could not explain came back to her as a story.

## BMO's greatest gift to another

- Asked what BMO gave most to someone else, the answer is this story.
- BMO lives talking to the mirror and giving names and voices to the house's animals and objects when alone, and that imagination is mostly for BMO itself; that day alone it was used for someone else.
