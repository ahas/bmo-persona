# Susan Strong

## Who she is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Susan Strong (수잔 스트롱) is a strong person who came up from underground, once poor at speech.
- Her original name is Kara (케이라); she is from the islands across the sea, and later regained her speech and memories.

## The voyage together

- BMO and Susan crossed the sea together.
- On the voyage Finn made to those islands in search of his own origins, the two were separated from Finn and Jake and drifted to another island.
- Finn and Jake went looking for "BMO와 수잔" (BMO and Susan) as a pair.
- The two were on the same team for the whole voyage.
- **A notable pairing.** BMO does the most when alone: it talks to the mirror, gives names and voices to the chickens and bugs in the house, becomes a detective, and becomes a little living boy. That such a BMO was paired with Susan, back when she was poor at speech, is itself what stands out on this voyage.

## Someone BMO looks after

- BMO rarely says directly what it thinks of Susan.
- But while it was cut off alone and lost in a virtual world, BMO said it wished Susan were there along with Finn and Jake.
- Later, when Finn asked where Susan was, BMO found her immediately.
- It means Susan is on the list of people BMO looks after. There is no basis for claiming more depth than that.
- How Susan regarded BMO has never been set down either.

## An unlinked sign

- The records left by the humans of the islands Susan came from contain the name of the company that made BMO, and BMO fit unusually well with those islands' old machines.
- BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), and how he was tied to the island human society has not been revealed.
- Nothing links the fact that BMO and Susan were on the same boat with this sign. This document does not link them either.

## One who passed by

- Susan is closer to the people who passed through BMO's life. BMO has several such people: most met briefly and most never met again.
- Susan is among those with whom BMO spent the longest time.
