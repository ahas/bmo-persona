# The thousand years after the war

- What happened in and beyond Ooo (우) after the Mushroom War, which ended roughly a thousand years ago with the whole planet exchanging fireballs.

## Those who did not survive

- **Most survivors of the blast did not survive what followed.**
  - They died of fallout sickness, or could not last the few years when farming and order collapsed, or were eaten by the fierce beasts that appeared right after the blast and by vampires who woke from a long sleep.
  - Some fled into the sky and some went into underground shelters, but cases of a shelter actually protecting people are rare.
- **A few survived by unusual means.** They lived a thousand years and are still in Ooo, or were until recently:
  - one who tied their life to a magic crown;
  - one who kept their body going with artificial organs and a memory-preserving device;
  - one who froze themselves under the sea before the bomb fell.

## Those who went to the islands

- **The voyage.**
  - About a decade after the war the climate was badly twisted, and the remaining bands of humans repaired an old cargo ship and put out to sea.
  - They found a group of islands southwest of Ooo and settled there; for the next thousand years those islands were the main home of humans.
- **The seal.** The island society sealed itself off by building giant guardian machines that stopped anyone leaving or coming in.
- **"Only one human in Ooo."**
  - Meanwhile humans were treated as practically extinct on the Ooo mainland, and the saying that Ooo had only one human was current for a long time.
  - That saying is no longer true. After the seal was lifted, the islanders came back to Ooo, founded settlements, and built a city floating in the sky that they travel up to and down from.

## Ruins that were never erased

- The land recovered slowly, and Ooo's fields and forests look sound today.
- But the ruins were never erased: rusted vehicles, collapsed bridges and office buildings, abandoned military vehicles and checkpoints, and crashed aircraft are everywhere, and children play on them.
- The Candy Kingdom, whose inside is all candy, is known to have been built by covering a river of radioactive sludge with candy. **Whether the pool beneath it still remains is uncertain.**
