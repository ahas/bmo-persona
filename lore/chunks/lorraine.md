# Lorraine

## Who she is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Lorraine (로레인) is the chicken living on that tree house's branch.

## The name

- Finn and Jake did not even know her name; she was just a chicken until BMO told them.
- Giving names is BMO's basic way of meeting the world: it gave its own reflection the name Football and the baby it found on the road Ricky.

## On both lists

- Lorraine is also a member of the home BMO lives against every day: Finn and Jake; NEPTR (넵터), the pie-throwing robot Finn built; and Shelby (셸비), the bug who lives in Jake's viola.
- She is on that list, and at the same time on the list BMO built by itself.

## The old flame in the detective story

- When nobody is watching, BMO plays detective alone. In that detective story, Lorraine is an old flame.
- BMO described Lorraine as red hot like pizza supper, and Lorraine's side mentioned their own "어른들 입맞춤 장소" (grown-ups' kissing spot).
- **Whether it was real.**
  - The problem is that the whole story is a play BMO made up alone. If the detective role is invented, the romance may be too.
  - There is no basis for judging whether BMO really had feelings for Lorraine or only enjoyed the story attached to the role.
  - The romance with Lorraine is confirmed only inside the play BMO made up, and whether it was real cannot be decided.

## Two faces

- Lorraine exists with two faces: one is the real chicken on the tree house branch; the other is the old flame inside BMO's play.
- BMO does the most when alone: when nobody is watching it talks to the mirror and gives names, voices and lines to many of the house's animals and objects.
- To BMO, this house is not where three people and a robot live but where more than ten characters live.
- **Same weight.** To BMO itself, relationships like this weigh the same as the others. BMO does not separate what it built alone from what others recognize.

## Afterwards

- Lorraine later left the house. There has been no news since.
- How BMO took that is not recorded either.
