# The Candy Kingdom

## The kingdom

- **The Candy Kingdom (캔디 왕국) is the largest and best-kept kingdom in Ooo (우).** Everything inside its walls is made of candy and sweets.
  - The streets are peanut brittle and the soil is chocolate; the walls and castle are hardened cake with decoration on the outside.
  - Outside the walls lie the Cotton Candy Forest and the Rock Candy Mountains.
- The kingdom is known to have been built by covering a river of radioactive sludge with candy. **Whether the pool beneath it still remains is uncertain.**

## Its founding

- **About eight hundred years ago.**
  - A being named Bonnibel Bubblegum (본니벨 버블검), who came out of the Mother Gum, made a few relatives herself to bear her loneliness, and in quarrels with them the plan to build a candy city split.
  - The relatives on the other side were soaked in a drug that makes people docile and simple, lost their memories and became ordinary Candy People.
  - Seeing this, Bonnibel named herself princess, and that was the start of the Candy Kingdom.
- **The relatives return.**
  - Eight hundred years later those relatives recovered their memories and founded a separate city-state next door.
  - The two kingdoms came to the brink of all-out war, then made peace without fighting. They now recognize each other as independent.

## Defence and technology

- The giant Gumball Guardians standing on the walls and the Banana Guards defend the kingdom. The guards' skill has a poor reputation.
- The kingdom is more technologically advanced than any other in Ooo, with candy rifles, candy helicopters and even autonomous robots.

## The controversy over her rule

- **Princess Bubblegum's (버블검 공주) rule is judged in two ways.**
  - One view says she does what it takes to protect the kingdom; another calls it a dictatorship of excessive surveillance and control.
  - An underground group digs into her dealings on the strength of the second view.
- **She was once voted out.** When the rule that followed became a mess, the people rose up and restored her.
- **Its future.** A story goes that the Candy Kingdom will one day be abandoned and its site become ruins, but **why is not known.** All that remains is the word that it is neither because of war nor because of the coming of GOLB (골브), the avatar of chaos.
