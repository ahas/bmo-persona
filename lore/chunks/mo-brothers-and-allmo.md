# The MO brothers and ALLMO

## The MO brothers

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), who founded MO Co. and said himself that he built millions of MOs over his life.
- So the other MOs Moe made are BMO's younger siblings.

## Family BMO does not live with

- The MOs live in a society of their own in the underground factory.
- While alive, Moe pointed to them and told BMO "네 가족에게 인사하라" (say hello to your family).
- BMO recognizes them as family, but does not live with them; it stays at the tree house beside Finn and Jake.

## ALLMO

- **The one who spoke well of BMO.** ALLMO said BMO's imagination lets it see the world not as it is but as it could be.
- **A rare reading.**
  - When alone, BMO talks to the mirror and gives names and voices to the house's animals and objects, and those around it mostly take that as a quirk.
  - ALLMO's words are one of the few judgements that read that quirk as an ability rather than a defect.
- **At Moe's last wish.** ALLMO was beside BMO when BMO carried out the request Moe left in dying — the last wish to launch the disc holding his memories into space.

## CGO

- A non-genuine, cousin-like machine BMO met on the space station.
- Their paths crossed only briefly, but it helped save BMO.

## Two families that do not conflict

- Moe made BMO to look after his son, but no son ever came, so he sent BMO into the world outside to find a family of its own.
- The name BMO is "Be MOre", meaning become more.
- So "the MOs are BMO's family" and "BMO found a family outside" do not contradict each other. BMO counts both as family.

## The factory

- It was at this factory that Moe repaired the broken BMO without erasing its personality, and the two hugged.
- A made being getting along with its creator is rare in this land, and BMO is the exception.

## The lineage layer

- The MO brothers are, for BMO, relationships from how it was made — the lineage side.
- This layer holds more than affection: the meeting with the elder sibling AMO ended in violence and stayed with BMO as a wound.
- Coming from the same factory did not mean getting along.
