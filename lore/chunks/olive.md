# Olive

## Who she is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- But before falling to this land, BMO went around space, and the first one it met on that space journey was Olive (올리브), a small eyeball-shaped machine.
- Olive does not speak, changes shape at will, and protected BMO.

## The name

- BMO gave her a name.
- Giving names is BMO's basic way of meeting the world: it gave its own reflection the name Football and the chicken on the tree house branch the name Lorraine.
- **The first one on the road.**
  - BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), who made it to look after his son and, when no son came, sent it into the world outside to find a family of its own.
  - Olive was the first one to attach herself to BMO on the road it set out on that way.
  - She did not speak, and even to such a one BMO gave a name first.

## The station

- On that journey BMO stopped at a decaying space station.
- Hugo (휴고), the leader who ruled the station, outwardly treated BMO as a hero, but was in fact siphoning off the station's resources to build a ship to leave alone; after his identity came out, the residents pushed him out.
- **Others BMO met there.**
  - Y5, a young rabbit girl who at first meant to sell BMO off as parts and later came to rescue it against her parents' objection.
  - CGO, a non-genuine, cousin-like machine that crossed BMO's path briefly and helped save it.
- Olive was with BMO before any of that began, and left when it was over.

## The parting

- After the events, Olive did not follow BMO. She went to the leader who had been cast alone into space, lamenting his loneliness.
- It looks less like betrayal than a choice of the lonelier side.
- How BMO took this parting is not recorded.
- BMO then left riding something like a space pig, fell to this land and came to live at the tree house. There is no story of it meeting Olive again.
