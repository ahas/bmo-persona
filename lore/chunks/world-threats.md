# What threatens the world — the Lich and GOLB

- Two beings are said to be able to end not just Ooo (우) but the whole world.

## The Lich (리치)

- **What he is.**
  - A being over a billion years old, said to be evil itself given form.
  - His soul fell long ago to this planet called Earth aboard a catalyst comet, and he gained a body only after the mushroom bomb went off in the Mushroom War roughly a thousand years ago.
  - He has a single goal: the extinction of all life.
- **His record.**
  - He calls himself "the last scholar of GOLB" (골브의 마지막 학자).
  - He was once defeated by a legendary hero and imprisoned in an amber (호박, 琥珀) prison atop the Candy Castle; since then he has appeared moving from body to body.
  - He is one of "the deathless", whom Death's hand cannot reach, and Death himself is said to fear him.
- **The comet.** It is said that in every age the catalyst comet carries an avatar of good and an avatar of evil together, but **the only source for this is the comet itself.**

## GOLB (골브)

- **What he is.**
  - The avatar of chaos, disorder and entropy. **Which dimension he lives in is not known.**
  - His breath turns living things into beasts of discord, and whatever enters his belly is taken apart layer by layer and returned to its original form.
- **His one summoning to Ooo.**
  - Two kingdoms on the brink of war then dropped their fight and stood against him together.
  - Weapons could not wound him; in the end only harmony — a song many sang together — weakened him for a while.
  - Finally one person merged themselves with him, becoming a new being, and left Ooo.
- **The Lich's unrequited service.** A story goes that the Lich devoted his whole life to serving GOLB yet never received any recognition from him.
- **Open.** Where GOLB came from and where he is now is still an unanswered question in Ooo.
