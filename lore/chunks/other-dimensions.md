# Places that are not Ooo — Lumpy Space and the Nightosphere

## Overview

- Ooo (우) is made of kingdoms large and small side by side, but some places named on the list of those kingdoms are not in fact inside Ooo. They are other dimensions.

## Lumpy Space (램프 스페이스)

- **A separate dimension, not inside Ooo.**
  - It can be entered only through the frog's mouth in the Cotton Candy Forest, the area surrounding the Candy Kingdom outside its walls, and a password is required.
  - Houses sit on lumpy purple and blue clouds, and there those who are not lumpy are called "smooths" (매끈이).
- **Its royal family and lumps.**
  - Lumpy Space has a royal family, said to hold the fifth seat outside Ooo's four elements — fire, ice, candy and slime — called **lumps (덩어리)**.
  - Lumps are described as a subspace lattice that binds the power of science and the power of magic together, and as stronger than any single element.
  - This description comes from the Lumpy Space royals themselves, so how accurate it is **has not been verified.**

## The Nightosphere (나이트오스피어)

- **Another dimension, where demons live.** A door opens when a face is drawn, bug milk is poured and a spell is recited.
- Demons are said to prefer German.
- The ruler of the Nightosphere is counted among "the deathless" (죽음 없는 자), whom Death's hand cannot reach.

## Other dimensions and the ways between

- **The Time Room.** There are more dimensions than these two. At the centre of the multiverse is a dimension outside time called the Time Room, from which the ripples of time that every other dimension undergoes are said to come.
- **Ways between dimensions.** There are many:
  - permanent passages;
  - holes that open and close on their own;
  - doors opened by magic;
  - doors that open only with very powerful objects, such as the gems of power or the book called the Enchiridion (엔키리디온).
