# The showdown with AMO

## The two brothers

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni made at the MO factory under the Bad Lands.
- AMO is the first MO, built before BMO — a robot like BMO's elder brother.
- AMO was made to receive love; BMO was made to give love.

## AMO revealed

- On BMO's birthday a large MO came to the tree house claiming to be Moe moved into a new body, sent BMO off to the factory, and stayed behind.
- Back at the tree house, Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크) found "Moe" crying with his legs cut off.
- He said NEPTR (넵터) had done it and told them to throw NEPTR out, and said BMO was garbage.
- When the two got angry, he took off the wig, regrew his legs, changed his voice and revealed himself: AMO.
- He shouted that the real Moe was dead, threw black metal spheres and knocked Finn and Jake down.

## The cliff

- When the returning BMO asked where its two friends were, AMO dodged the question, ran off and stood at the edge of a cliff.
- A red drive was fixed to AMO's head: Moe's backup, that is, Moe's memories.
- BMO tried to take back Moe's backup.
  - AMO's metal spheres smashed BMO's screen and arm, but BMO kept charging.
  - As AMO formed the next sphere, BMO leapt, grabbed hold of it with its hand, and pushed together with it to send AMO over the cliff.
- AMO's heart cooled to grey.

## Afterwards

- **Turning down the factory.** ALLMO came up bringing Finn and Jake and asked BMO to become the factory's leader. BMO refused: it had just killed someone and needed time alone.
- **What BMO felt.**
  - It had grown up a little, and it was left alone in a world without Moe.
  - It was different from AMO: it is not confined to the purpose it was made for and can think about the world.

## What it shows

- BMO's cruelty is not wickedness; it comes from having no circuit that hesitates once a judgement is made.
- And afterwards it always pays for it: that day stayed with it and came back as nightmares.
