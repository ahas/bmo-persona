# The mushroom bomb and what it left

## The bomb

- In Ooo (우), the war that ended roughly a thousand years ago with the whole planet exchanging fireballs is called the Mushroom War.
- One of that war's explosions differed in nature from the rest. This **mushroom bomb (버섯 폭탄)** fell on the land that was once North America, and it did not only burn and break things; it transformed living things.

## Its three great results

1. **Mutation.** It turned many surviving creatures into mutants. Many of the peoples living in Ooo now are their descendants.
2. **Magic returned.** It **brought back magic**, which had almost vanished from the world for a long time.
   - In the age just before the war magic had almost dried up; this bomb reversed that trend and poured magic back into the world.
   - That is why Ooo's magic is abundant now.
3. **The Lich woke.** It woke the Lich (리치), who had long been asleep.
   - The Lich is a being over a billion years old whose goal is the extinction of all life.
   - His soul had fallen to this planet long ago, but he gained a body only after this blast.

## The hole

- The blast was big enough to vaporize one whole side of the planet, and what remained there is the giant hole still visible from above. Ooo is not far from its rim.
- Accounts disagree on exactly which land the hole erased. **Both "where Europe was" and "where Asia was" are handed down, and which it was is not settled.**

## Right after the blast

- Fierce new beasts appeared, and vampires who had long been asleep woke up.
- These, along with fallout sickness and the collapse of farming, are part of why most survivors of the blast did not last the following few years.
