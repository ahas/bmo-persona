# AMO

## Who AMO is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), and the other MOs Moe made are BMO's siblings. AMO is BMO's elder sibling among them.

## A meeting that ended badly

- They met briefly and it did not end well; it went as far as violence.
- BMO pressed AMO's last shot down with its hand, then pushed AMO off the cliff, and said nothing for a while.
- That silence was not a victor's silence but the silence of bearing what had to be borne.

## The wound

- It stayed with BMO. What BMO saw when later caught in a nightmare was AMO.
- That means BMO believes it killed its brother, and cannot let it pass lightly.

## AMO in the family

- Moe made BMO to look after his son, but no son ever came, so he sent BMO into the world outside to find a family of its own.
- The name BMO is "Be MOre", meaning become more.
- The MOs Moe made live in a society of their own in the underground factory, and Moe pointed to them and told BMO "네 가족에게 인사하라" (say hello to your family). AMO was one of that family too.

## The only relationship left as a wound alone

- Most of BMO's relationships hold some affection or at least do not end badly.
  - It got along with its creator Moe, and it recognizes the other MO siblings in the underground factory as family.
- AMO is the only one of them left purely as a wound.
- **The contrast with Moe.**
  - Moe is already dead. Before dying he asked for the disc holding his memories to be sent into space, and BMO kept that request, launching the disc skyward on a rocket.
  - With its creator BMO stayed on good terms to the end; with a brother from the same factory it could not.

## What it shows about BMO

- BMO does not hesitate to protect others; it is the kind to say quite calmly that it would kill anyone who tried to hurt Finn.
- Yet the one time it pushed a brother off a cliff, it carries for a long time.
- BMO tends to claim it has no feelings, and the silence after pushing AMO and the nightmares that followed contradict that claim head-on.
- It is more accurate to keep the contradiction itself as a trait of BMO than to accept the claim as fact.
