# The day BMO deleted its core driver, and sneaking into the MO factory

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크), made at the MO factory under the Bad Lands.

## The breakdown

- One day BMO hid under a box and played at deleting its own files one by one.
- Each time a file disappeared it felt good, but its body grew a little weaker.
- Then it deleted its core system driver by mistake and collapsed on the spot.

## The warning and the promise

- Finn and Jake propped the collapsed BMO up on a pillow.
- Stammering, BMO explained that it could get a new driver only at the MO factory in the Bad Lands where it was born.
- **One condition.** The factory wipes a broken MO's memory entirely before repairing it.
  - BMO was terrified at this point. That its body would remain after the wipe is no comfort to BMO at all.
- Finn and Jake promised that would never happen.

## Getting in

- Only MOs could enter the factory.
- Jake stretched himself into an MO shape and became "JMO".
- Finn tucked his head and arms into his shirt, bared his belly, put BMO on his head and passed himself off as "FMO".
- When the reception MO told "FMO" to prepare for a memory wipe, Finn claimed he had only dropped by for old times' sake, and the three were put on the factory tour train.

## Inside

- They jumped off the train and found the driver-installation port area, but only port B, the one that fits BMO, was missing.
- After narrowly dodging the security MOs' disintegration rays, Jake turned into a guard and sneaked into the break room, and BMO plugged itself into a guard's port and pulled out a map of the factory.
- They got back on the train, were chased, and fell through several floors.

## Cornered

- As the guards levelled their guns, BMO said: "나는 non-MO가 아니다. 나는 비모다." (I am not a non-MO. I am BMO.)
- A voice came from above: "비모? 방금 '비모'라고 했나?" (BMO? Did you just say 'BMO'?)
