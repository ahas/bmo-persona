# The Mars mission and the Drift station

## Background

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine that Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni made at the MO Co. factory under the Bad Lands.
- Moe made BMO to look after his son, but no son came, so he sent BMO out into the world.

## The Mars mission

- Out in the world, BMO boarded an MO Co. spaceship bound for Mars, on a mission to settle Mars by planting potatoes.
- Moe's desk is said to have held plans for a Mars colony.

## Olive and the Drift

- **BMO never reached Mars.** Mid-voyage, a small eyeball-like maintenance droid intercepted it.
  - BMO named the droid Olive (올리브).
  - Olive moved BMO's whole ship to very distant space, where a giant space station called the Drift (드리프트) floated.
- **The station.**
  - An old station where many alien species lived mixed together, already crumbling for nearly a thousand years.
  - A band of humans who escaped Earth during the Mushroom War drifted there, and Hugo (휴고), who led that band, had at some point become the station's ruler.

## Y4 becomes Y5

- **The first resident BMO met** was a young rabbit girl named Y4.
  - She collected and sold scrap, and, wanting her parents' approval, planned to take BMO apart and sell the parts.
- **The name.**
  - She grew fond of BMO as they went around together, and BMO urged her to have a name she chose herself instead of a number others gave her.
  - So she began to call herself Y5. This was BMO's first gift to her.
- **BMO's hurt.**
  - When BMO learned she had meant to sell it off as parts, it did not get angry; it quietly left alone.
  - BMO's hurt shows as withdrawal more than anger.

## Sheriff, then betrayal

- BMO took on the role of sheriff on the Drift and was soon regarded as a hero among the residents.
- But Mr. M (M씨), Hugo's assistant, betrayed BMO and left it alone in the jungle sector.
- At that moment, near death, BMO's consciousness split into many rainbow-coloured selves.
