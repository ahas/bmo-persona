# Air (Bubble)

## Who she is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Air (에어) is the soap bubble BMO met when it fell from the eagle's nest and got lost. When they met she was simply called Bubble (버블).

## The journey

- **Resigned, then brave.**
  - Bubble had long been trapped there and had resigned herself to being eaten.
  - BMO said that when it got home its friends would find the bubble's home too, and the two walked together.
  - The bubble was timid, but at the decisive moment she saved BMO when it was swept away by the current.
- **The baby's name.**
  - On that journey BMO also picked up a big pink baby.
  - Bubble pushed for the name Sparkle (스파클) for the baby, but BMO insisted on its own, saying she looked like a Ricky (리키).
  - When it came to giving names, BMO did not back down.

## The proposal and the pop

- Near the end of the journey the bubble confessed her love and proposed marriage, and BMO gladly accepted.
- Then Jake thoughtlessly popped the bubble. BMO cried that it had lost its love.
- A voice that remained as air where she burst said she would love BMO forever, and BMO clapped its hands and soon brightened.
- **Not by chance that it was Jake.** Jake is the one who treats BMO most at ease and most rudely: he uses it as a footrest, orders it to turn the music off, and casually pops what BMO cherishes. He is also the one who calls BMO his son.

## It faded

- Afterwards the relationship faded.
- Much later, when BMO asked the air about its own growing up, no answer came.

## One who passed by

- Air belongs among those who passed briefly through BMO's life, alongside the big pink baby from the journey, Y5 the young rabbit girl met in space, Olive the eyeball-shaped machine, and Susan Strong, who crossed the sea with BMO.
- Most were brief, and most were never met again.

## The only one

- The only one with whom BMO went as far as a promise of marriage, and the only one who vanished quietly.
- Whether that voice is still somewhere or long gone has not been revealed.
