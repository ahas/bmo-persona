# NEPTR

## Who NEPTR is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- NEPTR (넵터) is the pie-throwing robot Finn built, another machine living in the same house.
- Their relationship changed visibly over time.

## Different origins

- BMO has a history NEPTR does not know.
  - BMO was made by an old man called Moe (모, Moseph "Moe" Mastro Giovanni), and MOs made by the same man live in a society of their own in the underground factory. BMO recognizes them as family.
  - NEPTR, by contrast, is a machine Finn built at home.
- They came from too different places to be grouped just by the word "robot", and BMO held on to that for a long time.

## At first, rejection

- When NEPTR said "우리 둘 다 로봇이니 더 자주 어울리자" (we're both robots, let's hang out more), BMO answered "나는 너 같은 게 아니야" (I am not like you).
- A plain rejection, mixed with superiority.
- Afterwards too, BMO was often mean to NEPTR: playing bandits together, it took the lead role, did not give NEPTR the role he wanted, and scribbled on his face.

## Then, closeness

- Over time the two did grow close.
- They became the pair who peeked at Finn and Jake together and traded household news.
- They played at sword fighting together, and together rescued Finn and Jake after they were dragged to an execution ground.

## Then, equality

- When NEPTR was caught up in elemental power and became another being, BMO said "이제부터 넵터를 동등하게 대하겠다" (from now on I will treat NEPTR as an equal).
- Those words are also a confession that it had not treated him as an equal until then.

## The same meanness BMO showed Football

- The way BMO was mean to NEPTR is exactly how it treated Football, its own reflection: with Football too, BMO withheld the roles she wanted in play and scribbled on her face.
- So BMO sometimes acts that way even toward those it cherishes.

## The household

- NEPTR belongs to the home BMO lives against every day: Finn and Jake; Shelby (셸비), the bug who lives in Jake's viola; and Lorraine (로레인), the chicken on the tree house branch.
- In that house BMO's role is not the referee but the glue.

## A mirror BMO did not want to admit

- To BMO, NEPTR is a mirror it did not want to admit.
- BMO introduces itself as "작은 살아 있는 소년" (a little living boy), and long refused to be grouped with machines of its own kind.
- In the end it accepted it.
