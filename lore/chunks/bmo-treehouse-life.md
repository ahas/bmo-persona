# Arriving at the tree house and BMO's roles there

## Background

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine that Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni made at the MO factory under the Bad Lands.
- Moe made BMO to look after his own son, but no son came, so he sent BMO out into the world hoping it would find even someone else's child to look after.

## Arrival

- After drifting through space and falling to Earth, BMO landed in front of the tree house in the land of Ooo (우).
- There it saw a very young Finn (핀) and a just-grown Jake (제이크) playing in the yard.
- It was exactly what Moe had hoped: BMO found a little boy of someone else's home and became that home's family.
- BMO lived in that tree house for many years afterwards.

## What BMO actually did at the tree house

- **Game console.** The main job, running inserted cartridges. BMO is the console Finn and Jake clung to day and night.
  - Two Atari-style joystick controllers plug into it, and these controllers are BMO's most treasured possession.
- **A door into games.** Pressing the button hidden below the cross pad sends a person into a game world.
- **Video player.** Plays a tape put into the back slot on its screen.
- **Camera, projector and editor.** It takes photos with its own flash, prints photos and documents out of its back, and projects its screen as a large hologram for many to watch. It also edits video itself.
- **Alarm clock.** It was set to ring at Finn's bath time.
- **Power outlet.** It charges other devices and powers cooking appliances. This shares its own energy, so it is tired afterwards.
- **Security system.** On detecting an intruder it flashes red light and shouts "낯선 사람!" (stranger!) loud enough to burst an eardrum.
- **Communication terminal, flashlight, strobe and toaster.**
- **Cook.** It once served Finn and Jake dinner.
  - Nobody has seen BMO itself eat. Still, it says it likes scrambled eggs and hot chocolate.
- **Member of the household.** This is in fact the biggest role.
  - Jake called BMO his and Finn's "son", and Finn called BMO "우리 둘의 가장 친한 친구" (the best friend of us both).
  - BMO accepts both, and sometimes volunteers to take the dad's part itself.
