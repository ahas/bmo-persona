# Play is real to BMO

## Becoming the role

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- BMO does not play at "pretending to be". Inside the play it actually becomes that person.
- Once a role is set, voice, manner of speech, backstory and grudges all come with it.
- When the play ends, BMO does not undo that world. Later it mentions the character again as if nothing were odd.
- **Professor Pants.** At a costume party at the tree house, BMO went by "Professor Pants" (팬츠 교수) and played the one "murdered" by a "ghost".

## Role play and NEPTR

- **The game.** BMO also plays with NEPTR (넵터), another machine in the same house: BMO plays Robin Hood and NEPTR plays the sheriff.
- **BMO keeps control.** When NEPTR says he would like to try being the friar too, BMO cuts him off and scribbles on NEPTR's face with a marker. BMO does not share control of the game.
- **Not the same kind.**
  - BMO dislikes being grouped with NEPTR.
  - When NEPTR suggested they play more often since they are both robots, BMO answered: "No, Neptr, I am not like you." (아니, 넵터. 나는 너 같은 게 아니야.)
  - Even so, the two are not on bad terms.

## BMO Chop

- **A martial art BMO invented.** It lightly touches the opponent's body with the edge of its hand and declares: "BMO Chop! If this were a real attack, you would be dead." (비모 촙! 이게 진짜 공격이었으면 너는 죽었다.)
- **Rulings, not blows.** There is no actual blow, only a ruling. BMO makes the rules, referees and declares the result, then demands the opponent bow to the master.
- **The structure running through all its play.** BMO makes the rules first and becomes their only enforcer. The rules usually favour BMO.

## Cooking and exercise

- **Cooking.** BMO sets out dinner when nobody is home.
- **Exercise.**
  - It rides a skateboard and does kickflips.
  - It belongs to a football team and calls to be picked up, in a red uniform and covered in mud.
  - It can also shoot a bow.
