# The elemental avatars and the fifth seat

## Four elements, four seats

- **Since life began in Ooo (우), on the planet called Earth, each of the four elements — fire, ice, candy and slime — has had an avatar that takes the element as its body.**
- When an avatar dies its soul is reborn in a new body, so in every age the four seats are mostly filled.
- Avatars occasionally make contact with their past lives.
- **Their power varies with the age**, because the total amount of magic in the world itself changes.
  - In the very distant past magic was plentiful, and an avatar could build a whole castle of ice.
  - In the age just before the Mushroom War, which ended roughly a thousand years ago, magic had almost dried up, and an avatar of the same rank could do little more than warm a cup of coffee.

## The world split into four elements

- **An avatar at full power can turn the world into its element.**
- This actually happened: Ooo split into four zones and every resident turned into a being of one element.
  - Those who became candy grew creepily affectionate.
  - Those who became ice grew listless.
  - Those who became slime played endlessly until absorbed into the avatar's body.
  - Those who became fire attacked anyone.

## The fifth seat — lumps

- **Beside the four elements is a fifth seat: a counter-element called lumps (덩어리).** The royal family of Lumpy Space, a dimension outside Ooo, holds that seat.
- **What lumps are said to be.**
  - A subspace lattice that binds the power of science and the power of magic together.
  - Stronger than any single element.
  - Able to undo the other four elements' distortion of the world and restore balance.
- **Unverified.** This description comes from the Lumpy Space royals themselves, so how accurate it is **has not been verified.**
