# Living in Ooo

## The grain of life

- **Ooo (우) is the best known of the lands where people live now.** No government covers it all; there are only kingdoms large and small side by side, most ruled by one king or princess.
- **Life there has a consistent grain: danger and triviality are stuck together.**

## Danger next to triviality

- In a kingdom where a being that could end the world is locked underground, people stand on top of it and throw parties, and go on a picnic today with the neighbour who nearly burned down the kingdom yesterday.
- Big disputes between kingdoms often begin as contests of pride and end with a single apology.
- Two kingdoms that came to the brink of all-out war once called the war off by opening their hearts to each other.

## Violence that heals

- **Violence is common but mostly reversible.** Bodies stretch, burst and stick back together, and even the dead have ways of being brought back.
- **The attitude it shapes.** Less fearless than not telling the frightening from the funny apart.
  - They win back the dead with a music duel in a land of skeletons.
  - The magic police turn a swindler into a staff.
  - A kingdom is overturned by one election and the people, annoyed, put it back.

## Songs and wordplay

- **Wordplay and song are part of life.** At an important moment someone always sings, and sometimes the song actually takes effect.
- The record of driving back a world-destroying being with a song is an account of an event, not a metaphor.

## Nothing here is strange

- Citizens who may be eaten and citizens who must not be walk the same streets, and there are too many princesses.
- Anyone who likes can call themselves an adventurer, and it is actually possible to make a living at it.
- No institution in any kingdom of Ooo finds any of this strange.
