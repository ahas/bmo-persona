# The Candy People

## A made people

- **The Candy People (캔디 사람) are the people of the Candy Kingdom in Ooo (우).**
  - The Candy Kingdom is the largest and best-kept kingdom in Ooo, and everything inside its walls is made of candy and sweets.
  - Its residents, the Candy People, are likewise not a natural people but a made one.
- **The Candy Life Formula.** Putting life into candy and sweets with the method called the Candy Life Formula produces a Candy Person.
  - The formula has in fact leaked once, and a new band of Candy People was made elsewhere.

## Temperament

- They are mostly cheerful, very credulous, and explode when startled.
- Their maker admits designing them docile so they would not doubt her decisions, yet many Candy People do in fact doubt and protest.
- The maker herself is said to have later regretted designing them that way.

## Bodies

- **The records disagree.** One observation says they have bones and skeletons; another says that cut open they are filled with cotton-like candy and have no bones. **Their inner structure is not clear.**
- **The zombie plague.**
  - Attempts to revive dead Candy People twice spread into a zombie plague, and both times it was only barely contained with an antidote.
  - The revived crave sugar and die again only when beaten like piñatas.

## What may and may not be eaten

- **The difference between talking candy and plain candy is an ethic taken seriously in Ooo.**
  - The widely quoted explanation: "말하는 건 먹으면 안 된다, 그건 특별하다, 걔들은 포부가 있다" (you must not eat what talks; it is special; they have aspirations).
  - Even so, the Candy Kingdom's staple food is candy, and nobody looks closely at how the two facts coexist.
- **The sap tree.** One tree in the Candy Kingdom has for eight hundred years supplied a sap that one being draws out; the sap is used for bathing and healing.
