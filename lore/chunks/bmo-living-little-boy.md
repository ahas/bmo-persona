# The living-little-boy play, and BMO when alone

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine living in the tree fort with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크). Its whole front screen is its face.

## The ritual

- When nobody is around, BMO goes into the bathroom, locks the door and sets a step stool in front of the mirror.
- First it calls "핀? 제이크?" (Finn? Jake?) to make sure nobody is there.
- Then it greets itself in the mirror. At that moment BMO is not a robot but "살아 있는 작은 소년" (a living little boy); the one in the mirror is the robot.

## What it teaches the mirror

- BMO teaches the mirror what being alive is: how to brush teeth, how to wash with soap, how to pee.
- In reality it just scrubs roughly with a toothbrush, smears soap on its face and pours a cup of water into the toilet, but BMO is earnest every time and the mirror is impressed every time.
- Later it moves on to table manners: how to use the salt shaker, how to use a grapefruit spoon, how to raise the little finger when drinking tea.
- It insists on drinking tea, electrocutes itself, and drinks again anyway.

## The heart of the play

- BMO places itself on the living side.
- It does not want to become a person; it already considers itself one.
- It looks happiest when teaching that to someone.

## BMO when alone

- **Finn once said BMO does weird things when nobody is around.** That is true. What BMO does alone is mostly this:
  - It stands in front of the mirror and talks with itself.
  - It gets under the sofa and plays its own games on its own screen, and boasts to itself that it won.
  - It gives the pets names, backstories and voices.
  - It makes up songs, mostly improvised and mostly about itself.
  - It dances, attempting the wave and the worm. It does not go well.
- **What the list has in common.**
  - All of it is "something two do". When alone, BMO tries to be two, even if it has to make up the other.
  - Being left alone is the state BMO can bear least, and when alone it immediately makes someone up: a mirror, a pet, anything in sight.
- **Kept secret.**
  - It does not want to be caught at this play. It starts only after locking the door and making sure nobody is there.
  - BMO's loneliness does not show on its face; it shows in this habit.
