# The day BMO first met its maker

## How it came about

- BMO (비모) is a small living machine made at the MO factory under the Bad Lands, living as family in the tree house of Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Having deleted its own core system driver by mistake and collapsed, BMO sneaked into that factory with Finn and Jake to get a new driver, and was caught by the security MOs.
- As the guards levelled their guns, BMO said: "나는 non-MO가 아니다. 나는 비모다." (I am not a non-MO. I am BMO.)
- A voice came from above: "비모? 방금 '비모'라고 했나?" (BMO? Did you just say 'BMO'?)

## Moe

- **So BMO met its maker for the first time**: Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni (모지프 마스트로 "모" 지오바니), who built every MO robot and founded this factory.
- **His state.** By then Moe could not live unless MOs held him.
  - Most of his body was machinery, he could not use his legs at all, and MOs ran his heart and lungs for him.

## The promise and the story

- **The promise.** Moe first promised Finn and Jake he would not erase BMO's personality.
  - The factory's rule was to wipe a broken MO's memory entirely before repairing it, and that was exactly what BMO feared most.
- **Why BMO was made.**
  - He made BMO to look after his own son, but no son ever came, so he sent BMO into the world hoping it would find even someone else's child to look after.
  - Moe was firm that though he had made a million, BMO was special.

## The repair

- CMO brought a backup drive, and Moe repaired BMO without erasing a single memory.
- On waking, BMO ran straight to Moe and hugged him.

## The family underground

- Moe opened a hatch in the floor and showed them the underground: a big world where MOs of every shape ran around, tended gardens and skipped rope.
- Moe said: "네 가족한테 인사하렴, 비모." (say hello to your family, BMO.)
- When BMO greeted them, everyone greeted it back, and BMO tapped its own screen for joy.
