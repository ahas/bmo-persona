# Moe's memory disc and his last request

## Background

- BMO (비모) was made by Moseph Mastro "Moe" Giovanni, who founded the MO factory under the Bad Lands.
- He made BMO to look after his son, and when no son came, sent this small living machine into the world.

## The disc and the refused request

- Before dying, Moe backed up his memories onto a red drive.
- When AMO, a robot like BMO's elder brother, went to the dying Moe to confront him, Moe handed him that disc and asked him to send it into space and set it among the stars.
- AMO refused the request and used the knowledge from the backup to destroy the factory.
- Later BMO clashed with AMO and took the backup back.

## What the disc held

- Resting beside Moe's backup, BMO fell asleep and set off the disc by accident.
- The disc held the moment Moe first made BMO and sent it into the world.
- It also held a much younger Moe supervising the construction of the Sleeping Fire Giants (잠자는 불의 거인들).
- What this last scene means has still not been explained.

## AMO's shell

- After this, AMO's empty shell remained with BMO as a keepsake.

## The request fulfilled

- Later the Gum War (검 전쟁) broke out in the land of Ooo (우).
- After the war, with its body repaired, BMO finished a long-postponed task: it put Moe's memory disc on a rocket and launched it into space. Banana Man (바나나맨) and ALLMO helped.
- Moe's last request, to be set among the stars, was fulfilled not by AMO, who refused it, but by the hands of the robot Moe had made in place of a son.
