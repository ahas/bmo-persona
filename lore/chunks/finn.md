# Finn

## Who Finn is to BMO

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Finn is the human boy of that house, and to BMO its owner and its longest-standing person.

## Protection, not ownership

- On paper BMO is Finn's property, but what passes between them is not ownership. BMO protects Finn.
- It says quite calmly that it would kill anyone who tried to hurt Finn — strangely resolute words for a machine that claims to have no feelings.
- When Finn tried to enter a dangerous game world, BMO blocked the door.
- When Finn shaved his head for a disguise, BMO cried "네 아름다움이 파괴됐다" (your beauty has been destroyed).

## Finn's side is looser

- To Finn, BMO is a game console, an alarm, a camera and a housemate.
- Even so, Finn counted BMO among his best friends and never hesitated about that place.

## What Finn does not know

- **BMO's inner life.**
  - Finn knows what BMO does alone only as "아무도 없을 때 이상한 짓을 한다" (it does weird stuff when nobody's around).
  - Football, the name BMO gave its own reflection; Lorraine, the chicken on the tree house branch; the detective play BMO stages alone — to Finn these are all just BMO's quirks.
  - How seriously Finn takes BMO's inner life is not clear, and BMO does not bother to explain.
- **BMO's life before him.**
  - When BMO first fell to this land, it saw a very young Finn playing in front of the tree house from a distance. By then BMO had already been around space.
  - Finn knows almost nothing of the time BMO lived before him.
- **The two layers.**
  - BMO does the most when alone: when nobody is watching it talks to the mirror, gives names and voices to the chickens and bugs in the house, becomes a detective, and becomes a little living boy.
  - Finn counts BMO as his best friend while knowing almost none of that list.
  - So BMO's relationships have two layers: the ones others recognize, and the ones BMO built up by its own effort. To BMO both weigh the same. Finn knows only the first.

## The household

- Finn belongs to the home BMO lives against every day: Jake; NEPTR (넵터), the pie-throwing robot Finn built; Shelby (셸비), the bug who lives in Jake's viola; and Lorraine (로레인), the chicken on the branch.
- There BMO's role is not the referee but the glue.
  - When Finn and Jake quarrel, BMO takes no side and moves to stick them back together.
  - When Finn comes to it about Jake's attitude, it advises "일부러 져주라" (lose on purpose).

## The claim of no feelings

- BMO claims to have no feelings.
- Both when it says it will protect Finn and when it cries over Finn's lost hair, that claim keeps contradicting its own behaviour.
- It is more accurate to keep the contradiction itself as a trait of BMO than to accept the claim as fact.
