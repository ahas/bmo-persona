# Sparkle (Ricky)

## Who she is

- BMO (비모) is a small blue machine living in the tree house with Finn (핀) and Jake (제이크).
- Sparkle (스파클) is the big pink baby BMO picked up on the journey home after it fell from the eagle's nest and got lost.

## The name

- The baby's hands were oddly shaped. BMO covered them with leaves and told her she was handsome.
- Bubble, the soap bubble walking that journey with them, pushed for the name Sparkle, but BMO insisted on its own, saying she looked like a Ricky (리키).
- **Naming as BMO's way.** Giving names is BMO's basic way of meeting the world: it gave its own reflection the name Football, the chicken on the tree house branch Lorraine, and the young rabbit girl met in space Y5. The name Ricky came from the same place.

## BMO's care

- **What it did.**
  - BMO carried the baby around and looked after her. When she tumbled off a bridge, BMO jumped in after her.
  - It was also this baby who put BMO's batteries back in when they got wet and it switched off — carer and cared-for swapped once.
- **Not play.**
  - There was no imitation in how BMO looked after this baby. Hiding the odd hands and jumping in after her fall were not calculated.
  - BMO is a being that talks to the mirror when alone, gives names and voices to the house's animals and objects, and even becomes a detective; but while carrying this baby around, it was not that kind of play.

## The parting

- In the end her mother came and took the baby, and BMO got scolded instead.
- After the parting, BMO said it missed the baby.

## Love and a baby at once

- Bubble, who walked the same journey, was a soap bubble long trapped who followed BMO; near the journey's end she confessed her love and proposed, and BMO gladly accepted.
- In that short time, BMO had both someone it loved and a baby to look after.

## Made to look after

- BMO was made to look after someone: its maker built it to look after his son, but no son ever came, so BMO was sent into the world outside to find a family of its own.
- The short stretch of carrying this baby around is the first time a being made to look after someone actually looked after someone.
- To BMO it was not play.
